# Gates: D3 — the ingest ledger (the no-silent-loss guarantee)

Scope: packages/hub/src/stream.ts, packages/hub/src/server.ts (frames ingest ledger record +
register-ack `ingested`), the agent's frame envelope gaining srcEpoch/srcSeq
(packages/agent/src/session.ts sink), the `sessiond_stream_gap` seam frame, and a property
test. Design §7 invariant: a line reaches the hub as a frame at most once per
(instanceId, epoch, srcSeq); a gap is always an announced reset, never a splice.

- [x] G1: property test sweeps agent-restart × hub-restart × sessiond-restart × ring-overflow and asserts at-most-once + no-silent-gap on every path
  CHECK: cd /home/bewinxed/cawco && bun test packages/hub/ packages/agent/ 2>&1 | grep -E "[0-9]+ fail" | tail -2
  EXPECT: 0 fail
  EVIDENCE: packages/hub/src/ingest.test.ts sweeps 64 cases — 2 agent-restart counts × 2 hub
  restart × 2 sessiond restart × 2 absence lengths (3 lines, and 20 > the sweep's ring bound
  of 8, i.e. overflow) × 2 live-line counts × 2 re-send tails; asserted at runtime
  (`expect(CASES.length).toBe(64)`). Each case asserts (a) no (epoch,srcSeq) and no child
  counter ingested twice, (b) consecutive ingested lines contiguous within one hub life and
  one sessiond epoch unless a `sessiond_stream_gap` frame, an epoch change or a hub restart
