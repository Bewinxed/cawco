### 2. ViewRoutines' band (ViewRoutines.svelte:922 and :974)

**The kit can't carry the class `band` itself.** A `ledger-band: "band border-0"` value fails `shadcn/no-unknown-classes`: `11:24 error "band" is not a class this project's Tailwind knows`. `@shadcn/lint` 0.2.1 (`dist/index.js:5042`) admits a non-Tailwind class only when the same file's `<style>` declares it, and ViewRoutines' does while the kit's doesn't.

**`band` is not a look but a hook.** Two rules in ViewRoutines' `<style>` hang on it:
- `[data-slot="ledger"] :global(tr.band) { background: var(--surface-band) }`;
- `:global(tr.band th) { block-size: var(--c-toolbar-ctl); padding-inline: var(--space-3); font: var(--type-label); letter-spacing: var(--track-caps); text-transform: uppercase; color: var(--ink-muted) }` (ViewRoutines.svelte:1149–1159).

They reach only these two header rows; ActionsTable's and RoutineDetail's band rows carry `border-0 bg-surface-band` on 1a and no hook. The kit already draws both looks:
- the row's is `variant="band"` (`border-0 bg-surface-band`);
- the heads' is `caps` (`type-label px-space-3 text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-caps`), with `h-c-toolbar-ctl` on the consumer, as every other band head has it.

**So the rows take `variant="band"`, the heads `caps`, and the hook and its two rules go** (set 4's edit, in its own file).

**`k1-exact.ts`** reads the hook as its rule's value (`AS_SOURCE`: `band` → `bg-surface-band`), so all 104 appendix rows are checked.

**`k1-modifiers.ts`, fixture `ViewRoutinesHead`.** It renders the routines table's head: today with the hook and the file's 1a `<style>`, and K1 with the mapping and that `<style>` minus the two rules.
