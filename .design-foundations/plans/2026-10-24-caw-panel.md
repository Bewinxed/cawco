## 1. Purpose, kept

Caw's panel is where you deal with Caw without leaving the page you are on. It is your conversation with him, with whatever waits on you at its top. You open it to answer him, to catch up and steer, to start new work, or to get him unstuck.

The purpose is unchanged. What changes is the construction: the three surfaces (his head's Needs-you list, Ask Caw's floating thread, the ⋯ with his switch) become one, and the panel's head bar goes.

## 2. The construction

One panel, five parts, top to bottom. Nothing else is in it.

| Part | What it is | Layer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Top zone** | A scroll edge effect over the content's first 40px (the house edge fade plus the panel material's blur at reduced opacity), no bar, no fill. Over it, at the leading edge, the **small title**; at the trailing edge, the **glass capsule**. | Controls float over content (HIG Materials) |
| **Stack** | Needs-you rows that belong to what is open, pinned under the top zone. Its foot is the one vermillion hairline (1px `brand-solid`, as the panel's sections part today). Empty, it has no height and no line. | Content (the panel's raised surface) |
| **Thread** | The large title (Caw's name for the thread) at the content's start, then the conversation. Needs-you items that happened in this thread stand in it as cards, where they happened. | Content, scrolls |
| **Can't-start block** | Only while Caw can't start: his hero still, what happened, the way out, Show details. Pinned above the composer. | Content, pinned |
| **Composer** | The message box, always. Composer glass, Send in coral. Caw peeks over its top edge on the panel's centre line (his rive state: idle, working, sleeping, needs-you). | Controls |

**Caw's axis (owner, 2026-10-24):** outside a row, Caw stands on the composition's own axis, never in a left lead column ("why is caw left aligned here? u seem to have left aligned him everywhere"). So: his peek over the composer is centred on the panel; his hero in the can't-start block is centred on the panel; the empty new thread's Caw is centred. Only inside a row (a message's speaker line, a docked row, a notice) does his mark take the 28px lead column, because there he is one of several marks on one grid.

### 2.1 Scope of the stack and the cards

A needs-you item is in scope when it belongs to what is open: this thread, a session of this project, this project (a task of kind `you`, an artifact waiting, a proposal), or the fleet (a machine waiting to be admitted, logins moved in). Nothing from another project is in the panel.

Two kinds of item:

- **Happened in this thread** (Caw's question, an Approve/Deny card for his call or a project setting, a proposal he asks about, a decision page he made and waits on): a **card** in the thread at its moment. When the card scrolls out above the stack's floor, it **docks**: its row unfolds in the stack. When the card comes back into view, the row folds away. A press on the row scrolls the thread to the card.
- **Happened elsewhere in scope** (a delegate's permission, a task card of kind `you`, an artifact waiting, an admit): a **row in the stack from the start**, no card in the thread, because it did not happen in this conversation. A press opens it where it lives (Peek on a desk for a session's ask, the session on a phone, the artifact at its first pending stop, the machines for an admit), as today's rows do.

Rows order longest wait first. A notice (a revision with nothing asked, a session that ended with its ask unanswered) is a row too, after the asks, with its ✕. The stack shows up to three rows; past three, a fourth line "and N more" unfolds the rest in place.

Caw's arcs on the bar count the panel's scope on this page: on a project's page, this project and the fleet; on the home, everything. See §7 for the one fork this opens.

### 2.2 The row (the stack) and the card (the thread)

The row is the panel's existing row recipe: a 28px lead column with the mark (a session's mark, Caw's, the artifact kind's glyph), the title in label type with its wait at the line's end in meta, the second line in meta (why it waits; for a permission, the tool and its argument), and the last control on the trailing edge `space-3` in. A permission's control is The Peer Rule's panel form: Deny then Approve, filled tinted pair, together at the trailing edge. A question's is Open. A notice's is its ✕.

The card is `permission-card` (surface-raised, `radius-lg`, 11px padding) on the thread's recess, with the same lead column, title, wait, and the same trailing pair; a question card holds its answer control (choices as option chips, or a field). Answered, the card morphs in place to its outcome line ("Approved · 14:02", "You answered: …"), and stays in the thread as history.

### 2.3 The top zone: title and capsule

**Title.** When Caw first answers, he names the thread in a few words (at most 4 words, 28 characters; HIG asks for under 15 characters on the small title, so the small title truncates with an ellipsis). The name stands at the content's start in the title role (`clamp(18px, …, 20px)`, 500), the large title. Once it scrolls past the top edge, the small title (label role, 13px, 500, `ink-strong`) stands at the top zone's leading edge over the content, `space-3` in, centred on the capsule's height. Before he has answered, the top is empty: HIG's "If titling a toolbar seems redundant, you can leave the title area empty", and the first message under it is the context.

**Capsule.** One glass group at the top trailing corner, `space-2` in from the panel's drawn top and trailing edge: the top bar's capsule recipe (panel material, hairline, tile shadow; solid raised with `border-contrast` under Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast) at `c-btn-h` 36, holding three borderless 28px items 4px in and 4px apart, each a 20px Solar duotone symbol in `ink-muted`, no dividers:

| Item | Symbol | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New thread | `add-circle` (linear plus) | Starts a new thread: the title clears, the thread empties, the composer keeps focus. |
| More | `menu-dots` | Menu: Caw on / Caw off (his switch, with its tray when something runs), Threads (the project's threads), Open as page, Clear all notices. |
| Close | `close-circle` (linear cross) | Closes the panel. Esc does too; on a phone, dragging the sheet down. |

Items hover to `surface-hover` and press to `surface-pressed` (The Press Rule), `--dur-control`. Each is a 44px touch area under a finger. No label text in the capsule.

Caw's state has no head line any more. It is said twice: by Caw himself over the composer (sleeping when off, breathing `working` while he works, `needs-you` when something waits) and by the composer's hint: "Message Caw" / "Caw is off · answers when you write" / "Caw is working…". A visually hidden status region says the same for a screen reader.

**The hero stills.** One per cause, edits of Caw's open-eyed source still through Backlot's edit-image, a day and a night version each (as `channel-stable.png` and `channel-stable-dark.png` are). The shared instruction, then each pose:

> Keep this exact character: the same crow, the same line weight, the same proportions, the same open eyes and calm expression, the same colours (`crow-ink` body, paper highlights). Change only the pose and one prop. Flat illustration, no gradients, no text, no background: transparent PNG, 960×720, the figure filling the middle 70%, its feet on the bottom third. The prop is drawn in the same line as the crow, in `ink-muted` grey, never in colour.
>
> - **No machine online:** Caw perched on the top edge of a laptop whose lid is shut, one wing resting flat on the lid, head turned to the viewer, waiting.
> - **Budget reached:** Caw sitting beside a small seed bowl tipped on its side, empty, one wing on its rim, looking at the viewer.
> - **Thread won't read:** Caw holding a sheet of paper up to his face with both wings, a corner of the sheet torn off, one eye peering past its edge at the viewer.

>
> Night version: the same drawing with the body's highlights lifted to `neutral-8` so the rim reads on roost-dark.

Only the still changes between causes. The block's frame, the lines' places and the buttons' places never move, so a cause that changes (no machine, then budget) swaps the still and morphs the words in place.

## 5. What moves when

Every transition turns back from where it is drawn (The Interruptible Rule). Under reduced motion every move below is a cross-fade over `--dur-fade`, and the sheet and the stack take their size at once.

| Moment | What moves | Duration · curve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open, desk** | The panel comes down out of the glass past its own height and its shadow's reach (the existing HangCard slide); the capsule and the composer come with it, no separate entrance. Focus lands in the composer. | `--dur-pop` 260 · ease-out |
| **Close, desk** | Back up into the glass. Esc returns focus to Caw's head. | `--dur-exit` 160 · ease-out |
| **Open, phone** | The sheet rises to its stop; released by a finger it settles on the house spring (0.4s perceptual, bounce 0). | `--dur-panel` 280 · drawer |
| **Close, phone** | Drag down past the threshold: the spring carries the finger's velocity out; × or Esc: slide out. | spring / `--dur-exit` 160 · ease-out |
| **Large title → small title** | Scroll-linked, not timed: over the last 24px of the large title's travel into the top zone, the large title's opacity runs 1→0 and the small title's 0→1 and its `translate` 4px→0. Reversing the scroll reverses it. | scroll-linked, linear in scroll |
| **Scroll edge effect** | Scroll-linked over the first 24px of scroll: the blur-and-fade band's opacity 0→1. At scrollTop 0 there is no band, so a thread read from its start has nothing over it but the capsule. | scroll-linked |
| **Caw names the thread** | The large title's slot unfolds from 0 to its height at the content's start; the words fade in. The scroller is anchored at the tail, so what the reader is reading does not jump. | unfold `--dur-pop` 260 · ease-out; words `--dur-fade` 200 |
| **A needs-you card lands** | As any row of the thread (The Nothing Jumps Rule): uncovered top to bottom while what follows makes room; its mark's ring pings one cycle, then rests. | arrive 260 · ease-out |
| **Card docks** | The card's bottom edge crosses above the stack's floor by 8px (hysteresis): the row unfolds at the stack's foot and the scroller's `scrollTop` moves by the row's height on the same frame, so the conversation under it stays put. No flight: the card is off screen, and an off-screen move jumps. | unfold `--dur-pop` 260 · ease-out |

### 6.5 Each of the three notes, three ways

The owner's standing rule: "with each desaign feedback i give u need to run /interface-craft ux ui critique, then /impeccable critique, and benji's family values, for each feedback". Family Values is read from benji.org/family-values (2024-07-08). Its rules, in his words: a tray "maintains focus on a singular piece of information or action per tray"; "each subsequent tray is designed to vary in height. This makes the progression or change unmistakably clear"; "If a component occupies a space and will persist in the next phase of the user's journey, it should remain consistent" (his rule against "redundant animations"); text that changes is morphed, "Continue into Confirm"; and "Delight through selective emphasis", which "depends on achieving a consistent level of polish everywhere".

#### Note 1 · One panel; needs-you inline, docking into the stack; only what is open

**interface-craft.** *Focusing mechanism:* the stack is the first thing under the top zone and its foot is the one vermillion line, so what waits is where the eye lands; nothing else at the top competes. *Progressive disclosure:* three rows, then "and N more"; a card's detail stays in the thread, a row carries two lines. *Redundancy:* a docked row and its card are the same item in two sizes, one visible at a time; the stack's heading is not written ("Needs you"), the line and the rows say it. *Consistency:* the row is the panel's row, the card is the transcript's card, the pair is the Peer Rule's. *User context:* the operator who came to answer finds the ask at the top whatever they scrolled to, and answering leaves them in the conversation. Issue found and fixed: a global item (an admit) as a card inside a project's thread would be a lie about where it happened; it stands in the stack only.

**impeccable.** Heuristic 1 (status) rises from 2 to 4: the count, the wait and the scope are on screen. Heuristic 6 (recognition) from 2 to 3: one door, but the switch is behind ⋯. Heuristic 4 (consistency) from 2 to 4. Cognitive load checklist: single focus yes; chunking yes (three rows); grouping yes (the line); one thing at a time yes (a row is one ask); working memory yes (the card stays as history). P1 found and fixed: Approve from a row with the card out of view, answered by the row's two lines and Open for a question. Persona Casey: the first row's pair is in thumb reach on a 390 phone.

**Family Values.** *One thing per tray:* the panel is one tray with one subject, the conversation; the stack is not a second tray, it is the conversation's own shelf, and a row is one ask. *What persists stays put and transforms in place:* a card that scrolls out does not vanish and reappear as a row; the row unfolds where the stack's foot is while the conversation under it holds still (the scroll compensation), and the reverse folds it back. A card that is answered morphs into its outcome where it stood; it never leaves and comes back as a line. *No redundant animation:* the dock has no flight, because the card is off screen; a flight from nowhere is the duplicate Benji names. *Selective emphasis:* one ping, on the card's mark as it lands, and nothing after it moves.

**Where the can't-start block stands.** The CawCo rule says a failure takes the surface's centre; the note says the hero stands above the message box. On an empty thread these are the same place. On a thread with messages:

- **A. Above the composer, pinned (recommended).** The thread stays readable above it; the eye lands on the block right where it would write. One place, so a cause that changes swaps the still in place (Family Values: what persists stays put and transforms in place).
