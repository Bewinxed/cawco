# Design System: CawCo

**The token source is `design/tokens/cawco.tokens.json`** (W3C DTCG). It is the one place a visual value is defined. `bun run tokens` in `apps/dashboard` builds it with Style Dictionary into `apps/dashboard/src/lib/tokens/tokens.css`. That is a `@layer theme` block where every colour that differs by scheme is `light-dark(light, dark)`. `app.css` imports it and defines no token of its own; its `@theme inline` blocks only bridge tokens to Tailwind utilities. The same command, from the same Style Dictionary config (`apps/dashboard/scripts/build-tokens.ts`, its Apple platform in `build-tokens-apple.ts`), writes the native apps' tokens into `apps/apple/Packages/CawCoKit/Sources/CawCoDesign/`: `Resources/Tokens.xcassets`, one colour set per colour token with Any and Dark appearances (Display P3, gamut-mapped), and `Generated/Tokens.swift`, with `Palette` colour accessors and `TypeScale`, `Space`, `Radius`, `Size`, `Effect` and `Motion` constants (points, seconds, `Font.Weight`, `UnitCurve`, `TypeRole`). Shadows, gradients and transitions stay web-only. A token's `$value` is its light (or only) value, and `$extensions["dev.cawco"]` carries `dark`, `coarse` (pointer: coarse) and `compact` (coarse or under 640px) variants. To change a value, edit the JSON and rebuild; never edit `tokens.css` or add a literal to a component.

The frontmatter above restates the shipped colours as the browser computes them, in both schemes, for tools that read DESIGN.md. Where it and the JSON disagree, the JSON wins.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Ledger, re-skinned for Caw&Co"**

CawCo is a board an operator glances at between other work. It answers one question: does anything need me right now? The design treats that glance like reading a departure board. Rows hold still, numbers line up, and a quiet row has to mean a healthy row. The copy is a calm operator's log, not a personality brand (`WORDS.md`).

The direction (Caw&Co "3b · Coral action") keeps the shipped ledger's density, metrics, spacing, radii, motion and Solar icons, and re-skins its colour and type. The surface is warm paper by day (neutral hue ~85) and roost-dark at night (hue ~70). Chroma stays low in all chrome. Colour is spent on five things only: the coral action (where you act), status, identity marks, the coral focus ring, and data that crosses a threshold. Depth comes from a recessed frame holding raised content, not from heavy shadows. The density is set by the operator's job: 44px rows, a 32px header band, 90px stat tiles and the 14px body size.

Neither scheme is the original. The theme class on `<html>` (resolved from the reader's choice or the OS by `theme.svelte.ts`) sets `color-scheme`, and every colour token picks its side through `light-dark()`. A design is drawn and checked in both, never light-first or dark-first.

Motion is continuity, never decoration. Something that exists on both sides of a change travels to its new place rather than vanishing and reappearing, and a list that changes reflows rather than jumps. Nothing moves after it has landed unless it is live work, and live work breathes slowly. The code rejects: ease-in curves, `rgba(0,0,0,…)` shadows, weights above 500, indigo, and hue used for decoration.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A coral action on warm paper; colour elsewhere only where it carries meaning.
- The frame recesses; content sits raised on it.
- Figtree for the product, JetBrains Mono for code, paths and IDs, Fredoka 600 for brand display only. Product weights 400 and 500 only.
- A spacing ladder measured off the reference comps (4, 7, 11, 14, 18, 21, 25, 32), not a 4px grid.
- Solar bold-duotone icons throughout, linear only for chevrons and arrows.
- Motion that keeps one object on screen through every change, honours reduced motion everywhere, and exits faster than it enters.

### Motion grammar

All motion stands on three curves and a short duration scale (values in the sidecar's `extensions.motion`).

- **Ease-out** (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`) carries entrances, exits and any change in place.
- **Ease-in-out** (`cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`) carries things already on screen moving from one place to another: rows sliding, a thumb gliding between segments, a height tween in the transcript.
- **Drawer** (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`) carries floating surfaces, sheets, page swaps, shared-element flights and container size changes.

**The No Ease-In Rule.** No ease-in curve exists. Anything that starts slow feels like the interface hesitating.

**The Fast Exit Rule.** Exits are shorter than entrances. A popover opens over 260ms and closes over 160ms. A dialog opens over 280ms and closes over 160ms. A toast rises over 280ms and leaves over 160ms.

**The One Object Rule.** What is on both sides of a change travels; it never vanishes on one side and appears on the other (`motion/share.svelte.ts`). A sidebar row becomes the tab it opens, a section row's title flies into the editor header, a sent message flies from the composer into the transcript row it became. A flight runs 280ms on the drawer curve, as a transform (FLIP) or a clip that opens from the source's box. The source is hidden while its copy flies, so the screen never shows two.

**The Nothing Jumps Rule.** When a list changes, rows that stay slide to their new places, rows that leave close while fading, and rows that arrive are uncovered top to bottom while what follows makes room (`motion/rows.svelte.ts`). Movement takes 280ms on ease-in-out, arrivals fade over 260ms, departures fade over 160ms, all started on the same frame. A move that happens entirely off screen jumps instead of sliding, so nothing drifts into view behind the reader's scroll.

**The Interruptible Rule.** Every transition turns back from where it is drawn. Popovers transition on `data-state` rather than playing keyframes, folds animate from the drawn height, and a segmented thumb caught mid-glide restarts from where it stands.

**Springs.** Surfaces released by a finger settle on one spring (`motion/spring.ts`): perceptual duration 0.4s, bounce 0, mass 1, stiffness `(2π / 0.4)²` ≈ 246.7, damping `4π / 0.4` ≈ 31.4 (critically damped). The spring is integrated once at release at 120Hz and handed to the compositor as keyframes about 8ms apart, carrying the finger's velocity. A tab track scrolled with a mouse wheel glides on a second, critically damped spring with a 0.3s response (stiffness `(2π / 0.3)²` ≈ 438.6, damping `4π / 0.3` ≈ 41.9).

**Live work breathes.** Anything still running pulses its opacity on `--breath` (2s, ease-in-out): the catch-up dot, a subagent's beat, the in-flight tool glyph, the Working status glyph. Nothing that has finished moves.

**The One Loop Rule.** Anything that turns or blinks while something is pending runs one ambient loop, `--dur-loop` (1.2s): a spinner, the task ring's arc, the supervisor's orbit, the skeleton's sweep, and Tailwind's `animate-spin`, `animate-pulse` and `animate-ping`, which the theme points at the token. A rotation turns linearly; a pulse runs ease-in-out and a ping ease-out. Longer rhythms are multiples of the breath, never new numbers: the thinking indicator's words change every two breaths. Tailwind's default transition timing (`transition-colors` and its siblings) is `--dur-control` on ease-out.

**The Reduced Motion Rule.** Motion is opt-in. Every travelling transition and keyframe sits under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and JavaScript motion asks the same question through `motionOk` (`motion/curves.svelte.ts`). With reduced motion, opacity and colour fades still run, pages cross-fade in place over 120ms, heights land at once, folds fade instead of opening, list changes only fade, flights do not fly, and loops stop (an indeterminate spinner keeps turning). Reduced motion means stillness. It does not mean an instant swap.

**Pages.** The shell keys its page on the route (`motion/route.svelte.ts`). Between two sidebar spokes the page moves 8% vertically, in the order the sidebar runs, over 120ms. Deeper or shallower moves nudge 8% horizontally. Browser back and forward follow the history step and flip in right-to-left layouts. On a phone, Configure behaves as a stack: a deeper page pushes in from the inline end over 280ms while the page it leaves falls back 30% and dims to 0.6, and back reverses it over 260ms. On a wide screen, Configure sections rise or drop 4% over 140ms while the rail holds still. Everything runs on the drawer curve, and the leaving page is inert.

**Theme switch.** The new theme is drawn at once under a wash of the old background, which fades off over 200ms on ease-out (`theme.svelte.ts`, `.theme-wash`). Element transitions are switched off for the flip. With reduced motion the theme flips with no wash.

### Sound and touch

**The Quiet Feel Rule.** Sound and touch accompany a gesture the reader is making; they never announce anything on their own, never stand in for a word or a glyph, and never play for something the reader did not do. Sound is Cuelume (synthesized live, no files), always at its `subtle` emphasis. It is off until the reader turns it on with the Sound switch beside the theme in the rail's footer, and the choice is kept in this browser (`feel.svelte.ts`). Touch is web-haptics: the Vibration API on Android, and on iOS Safari, which has none, the system tick of a switch toggled out of sight. It answers only a finger on a coarse pointer and has no setting. Both libraries load on the first press or key, never with the page, and Cuelume's audio graph is built while idle right after with an inaudible play, so the first cue costs no frame (built on the first real cue it cost the frame that cue landed in: 76ms against 4ms warm). The moments, all in the composer today:
- **Detent** (the recall wheel landing on another entry): `select`, rising toward older and falling toward newer, with a `selection` tick. Overshooting past an end is not a detent.
- **Hold** (a held press bringing the wheel up): `open`, with a `medium` tap.
- **Open and close** (the wheel brought up by a key or the history button, and put away unchosen): `open` and `close`, no touch.
- **Take and give back** (a queued message's words lifted into the composer, and given back to their bubble): `select` and `close`, each with a `light` tap.

**The Reserved Hue Rule.** Crimson, spark yellow, olive and blue mean status, and status only. Progress rings, identity and decoration take `currentColor` or an identity hue. A finished task ring is the one moment a decoration earns a state colour, because by then it is a result (`TaskRing.svelte`).

**The Coral Is Where You Act Rule.** Coral marks the action, the selected nav row, the focus ring, checked controls, the chosen option and the selection tint. It is never a status: failed is crimson and needs-you is spark, so coral never reads as an alarm and an alarm never reads as a button.

**The White On Coral Rule.** Text on coral sits on `action-solid` or `chip-chosen-bg`, the measured fills, never on `brand-solid`. A new coral fill under text is measured to 4.5:1, top gradient stop included, before it ships.

**The Ink Stays Ink Rule.** Tooltips and inverted tiles are `ink-solid` with `on-ink`. They are never coral.

**The Idle Has No Fill Rule.** An idle session carries no chip. A quiet row means a healthy row, so rest is drawn as nothing.

**The No Indigo Rule.** Indigo is never a brand, action or accent hue. Identity hues span the wheel for telling things apart and are never promoted to a brand role.

**The Glyph, Word, Hue Rule.** Status is never colour alone. ## Typography

**UI font:** Figtree Variable (`@fontsource-variable/figtree`, with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif), at 400 and 500 only
**Mono font:** JetBrains Mono Variable (`@fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono`, with ui-monospace, SF Mono, monospace), ligatures off

- **Prose** (400, 14px, 1.55): transcript prose and rendered markdown.
- **Title** (500, 18→20px, 1.25, −0.01em): page titles, dialog titles, empty-state titles. It scales with the viewport between 390px and 1024px.
- **KPI** (500, 20→24px, 1, tabular): a stat tile's figure. It scales over the same range and drops to the title size under 900px.
- **Column head** (500, 13px, uppercase, 0.06em tracking): a ledger's header band.
- **Code** (JetBrains Mono 400, 12px, 1.6, ligatures off): code, commands, paths, tool names, keycaps.
- **Measured figure** (`mono-figure`: JetBrains Mono 400 at its line's size, ligatures off, tabular, `font-size-adjust: cap-height` to `mono-figure-cap`, 0.703): a measurement standing among Figtree words, as the usage popover's and strip's times, week shares, session counts and hour ticks, and the Usage page's sentences, key lines, running counts and spend line. Unadjusted at the same step, Mono's digits stand 4% taller than Figtree's (cap 0.734 to 0.703em) and its x-height 12.5% taller; adjusted, its digits are Figtree's height (8.62px to 8.63px at 12px). It goes on the figure's own span, since a role's `font` shorthand on the same element resets the face. Names, heads and tooltips stay Figtree, as do figures in a role of their own (the KPI, a tile's title-size figure: their recipes name Figtree) and columns of figures (tables and lists, tabular Figtree by the Tabular Figure Rule).
- **Display** (Fredoka 600, 40px): brand only, the one place weight goes above 500.

The three dense roles (meta, label, body) keep one size at every width because they are already the smallest that reads. Rendered markdown restates its scale from these roles: h1 and h2 take the title size at 1.2 leading and −0.008em, h3 to h6 take the body size, and `strong`, `th`, `dt` and `summary` take 500.

### Named Rules
**The 500 Ceiling Rule.** No product text goes above 500; Fredoka's 600 is brand display only. Emphasis is a weight step from 400 to 500 or an ink step, never bold. The prose plugin's 600 to 800 weights are overridden back to 500.

**The Role, Not Size Rule.** Text takes a role by what it does. Names, group headings, column heads and badges are label. Sentences and descriptions are body. Timestamps, counts, units and hints are meta. Nobody picks a size and finds a role for it.

**The Tabular Figure Rule.** A figure that changes (a count, a price, an age, a percentage) carries `.num` and is set in tabular numerals, so a ticking value never moves its neighbours. Tables get tabular numerals by default. The KPI role always carries `.num`, because a `font` shorthand resets `font-variant-numeric`.

**The Truncate Inside Rule.** Text truncates with an ellipsis in an inner span, never on the control or link itself, so the hit area is never clipped. In a row, the name gives way first and the meta line keeps one line.

**The 16px Field Rule.** On a phone or a coarse pointer every text field renders at 16px (an `!important` in the base layer), because iOS Safari zooms into any field under 16px.

**Balanced wrapping.** Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. Paragraphs and list items use `pretty`.

## Layout

The shell is a resizable rail (228px by default, the reader's own width wins), a 44px top bar with a 1px hairline at every width (the compact bar's height, Apple HIG Toolbars), and a page on the recessed field. A page pads 25px on the inline start and 21px on the end, and the groups of a page (stat row, attention queue, cards) stand 18px apart. A page head is 96px from the bar to the first group, with the title over its one-line description and the actions centred on the pair.

**Spacing ladder.** 4, 7, 11, 14, 18, 21, 25 and 32px, measured off the reference comps. It is deliberately not a 4px ladder.

**Density.** Management surfaces run the comps' density: a 44px row pitch, a 32px header band, a 55px toolbar zone (with 32px toolbar controls on the fleet board only) and 90px stat tiles. The transcript runs a compact scale from the same tokens. Under a coarse pointer, compact relaxes to the touch floor.

**Fleet board.** Four stat tiles in a grid (two columns under 900px), the attention queue, any machines the board has something to say about, then the sessions ledger. The ledger uses a fixed table layout: the name column takes what the fixed columns leave (machine 140, harness 110, turns 64, context 76, last active 132, state 124, actions 124) and truncates. Under 640px each row becomes a two-line card: the title across the row, then machine · state · age, with the actions at the end.

**Transcript columns.** Every transcript row lays out on four edges measured from its own inline start: a 2px rail at 7px, a 16px glyph cell 11px past the rail, and the text column 7px past the cell. A nested transcript (a delegate's or a subagent's) starts at its parent's text column and repeats the edges. Under 900px the rail sits at 0. The transcript pads 25px / 21px inline (18px under 900px) and clears the floating composer at its foot.

**Composer column.** At most 720px wide, centred, docked 14px above the bottom plus the safe area. Under 900px it goes full width with 11px margins.

**Breakpoints.** 640px (phone: board rows become cards, toasts move to the top, the new-session dialog becomes a bottom sheet, popovers widen to the viewport less 24px), 900px (the rail becomes a burger sheet, the transcript rail moves to 0, the composer goes full width, stat tiles go to two columns), and 1024px (where the fluid title and KPI sizes reach their maximum). A coarse pointer counts as narrow for nav rows (44px) and for hosting the tab strip.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal first and shadowed second. The frame (app field, top bar sheet, dialog tray, header band, segmented groove) recesses. Content (cards, rows, menus, the dialog body, a segmented thumb) sits raised on it. Shadows are warm ink tints: by day the ink at low alpha, at night the deepest surface at high alpha plus a 1px inner ring of ink (`night-ring`, transparent by day, ink at 7% at night). Every shadow token adapts through `light-dark()` on its colours; none is keyed on the theme class.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Tile** (`0 1px 2px` ink at 7% by day / black at 30% at night, plus a 1px ring of ink at 3.5% / 6%): cards, machine tiles, icon tiles, report cards.
- **Hairline** (`0 3px 7px -2px` shadow-tint-2): a drop under a tile without a ring.
- **Stat** (the hairline drop plus the night ring): the stat tile and the permission card.
- **Raised** (`0 1px 3px` and `0 1px 1px` shadow-tint, plus the night ring): a thumb in a groove. At night the ring makes it read as a hairline-edged lift.
- **Overlay** (`0 18px 48px` shadow-tint-3, `0 2px 6px` shadow-tint, plus the night ring): popovers, menus, the dialog tray, toasts, floating tags. The modal shadow is the same value.
- **Drawer** (`0 12px 40px` shadow-tint-3): sheets.
- **Drop** (`shadow-drop`, `0 14px 28px` shadow-tint-2, then `shadow-drop-near`, `0 2px 4px` shadow-tint): a drawn shape rather than a box, set as two `drop-shadow()` filters so it follows the shape's edge: the grown composer, once it stands still.
- **Action ring** (`action-ring`: a white top hairline at 18% and a coral-tinted drop) and **code ring** (`code-ring`: an inner vermillion hairline and glow) are in the token source for the action and code blocks.

The Tailwind ladder has four steps, `shadow-xs`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg` and `shadow-xl` (Tailwind's own scale is cleared, so no other step exists). md, lg and xl carry the night ring.

### Materials
- **Scrim**: the page's own background at 72% with a 2px blur, behind dialogs and sheets.
- **Panel material**: the raised surface at 72%, with a 28px blur and 1.6 saturation, for chrome that floats over content. It turns opaque under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`.
- **Composer glass**: the panel material at the base blur (`--material-blur`, 20px, and 1.6 saturation), so the transcript shows faintly under the field; opaque raised surface under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`.

### Named Rules
**The Frame Recesses Rule.** The frame recesses and content never does. The one sanctioned card-in-card is the stat tile's recessed well, which the reference plan calls "a signature move of the design".

**The Warm Shadow Rule.** Shadows are warm ink taken from the neutral ramp, never `rgba(0,0,0,·)`. The tile's night drop is the one black, at 30%, under a ring of ink.

**The Night Ring Rule.** At night a raised surface is told apart from its frame by a 1px inner ring of ink as well as its drop. The ring is part of the shadow token, so a component that takes the token gets it in both schemes without a class.

## Shapes

Corners are soft and graded by role: 2px (`--radius-hair`) for marks too small to round further (chart swatches, a chart tooltip's key, the tooltip's arrow), 5px for marks and tiles, 8px for items inside a surface, 10px for controls and buttons, 12px for cards and menus, and 18px for the dialog tray. The stat well uses 7px. A fully round shape is kept for state pills on the board, switches and status dots; buttons are never pills.

**The Concentric Rule.** A surface nested inside another takes the outer radius less the inset. The dialog tray (18px, 6px padding) holds a 12px body. The composer shell (12px, 7px inset) holds 5px controls, and the autopilot's halo 3px outside its control is drawn at 8px. A subagent's well (8px, 4px padding) holds a 5px report.

**The Measured Exception Rule.** The stat tile keeps the comp's measured radii (a 10px card around a 7px well, 7px in), even though a concentric well would be 3px. The assistant panel keeps its measured 16px shell (`--radius-panel`, JOURNEY.md §Assistant), and the smallest marks keep a 2px hair (`--radius-hair`) rather than the 5px that would turn them into dots. Each is a token, never a literal.

**Borders are graded.** `border-hairline` separates rows and regions. `border-control` draws a control's edge. At night the generic border becomes ink at 8% (a seam) and the input edge becomes ink at 14%.

**Folder tabs.** Folder tabs are a drawer of real folders: the chosen tab comes forward from the horizon and the others recede and fold back (owner: the selected tab "approaches from the horizon when it's selected, while the others recede and fold neatly, almost 3d like"). A chosen folder tab is a sheet in the surface below it, with rounded shoulders and a foot that flares outward into the page (a CSS `shape()` path at the tab radius). Unchosen tabs stand on the shelf in `surface-recess-deep` (`tab-recede-1`, every one of them one step back, on the desk and on the phone's row alike) with rounded tops, overlapping their neighbours by the flare. The card under the chosen sheet is the sheet's own surface. The session tab's sheet is `tab-sheet` (the transcript's own surface, on every status), and the transcript's head dissolves into it: rows scrolling up under the strip blur progressively over `c-head-fade` and, on the same inOutCubic curve (`head-tint-ramp`), become the sheet, whole at the shelf's line, so the transcript and its tab read as one sheet. It comes in over the first `c-head-fade` of scroll and is absent at scroll top.

- **Lean.** Every tab is hinged on its foot, the shelf's line. The chosen tab stands upright; every other leans back 18° (`--lean`, fluid-tabs) as a pure `rotateX` with no perspective about its bottom centre, so it lowers its top by about 1.6px on the desk's 32px tab and moves nothing sideways: its foot, its sides and its overlaps hold. Titles and controls stay upright and drop with the card's middle. A receded tab under a fine pointer gives up 60% of its lean (`--lean-lift`) at the control tier. On the phone's 36px row the lean is faint and the fills do the work.
- **Slopes.** The card, the sheet and the rim bring their top corners in from the foot by `--slope-start` 1px on the leading side and `--slope-end` 0px on the trailing side (the owner's pick), chosen tab and receded alike.
- **Stack.** Tabs overlap in depth rather than sharing a stroke: every card runs its full width with both shoulders round, and the tab nearer the choice covers the one behind it. The tabs make no stacks of their own; each tab's card, sheet and contents take its rank in the track's one stack, and the rims stand above every tab. The rank follows how far back each tab stands now, read off the switch's own clock each frame, so two tabs trade places only at equal depth, when they look the same (transitions.dev, banner stacking: "the swap happens at identity"); ties go to the order the choice ends in.
- **One clock.** A choice is two states trading places where they stand, and every part of it travels together over `--dur-settle` (400ms, the owner's "Slow · 400ms") on `--ease-crossfade`: the lean and the contents' drop (transform, translate), the sheets (the new one fades in where it stands while the old one fades out; nothing slides or wipes), the card from the receded fill to the sheet's (background-color), the title's ink (the name twice in one cell, the strong ink faded in over the muted), the rims' strengths and their feet (opacity), and the chevron. Only transform, opacity and background-color animate, the transitions on the compositor; the stack's ranks, the seams and the rims' strengths follow the same clock frame by frame, read off the sheets' fade. `ease-crossfade` (CSS ease-in-out) moves at most 7.2% of a change in any 1/60s at 400ms; every other house curve moves more than an eighth in one frame there, which reads as a lurch. A swipe drives all of it frame by frame with the finger. With reduced motion the choice is instant.
- **Edges of a scrolling strip.** A strip that scrolls fades the tabs past its ends (the house edge fade), but never a tab drawn chosen: each end's fade is cut short at the chosen tab, and while a switch runs, at the tab it is leaving too, which is still drawn chosen until it lands. A choice scrolls the strip only as far as it takes to show the new tab whole, clear of the fades where there is room, and keeps the tab it leaves whole beside it where the two fit the strip together; where they do not (two tabs at their 240px cap on a phone), the new tab is the one shown whole.

**Session tab status rim.** Every session tab, at every width, wears its session's status on its own outline: working `status-live-glyph`, needs you `tab-rim-attn`, failed `status-fail-glyph`, idle and done `ink-muted`. The chosen tab wears it whole. An unchosen tab wears a quieter one, at `tab-rim-mix-subtle`, when its session is anything but idle (working, needs you, done, failed), and none at all when it is idle: no top, sides, feet or glow (owner: the tabs other than the selected tab "should have no rim", "unless they are active, then they should have a more subtle rim", "or any other status"). The stroke is `tab-rim-width` (1px, the owner's tuning) on the tab's whole outline, and the shoulders, the feet's narrowing, the glow and a gliding seam's stop all follow it: across the top, round both shoulders, down both sloped sides to the shelf, and at the foot straight down on a receded tab or out round the sheet's flare on the chosen one, with a 6px glow following the outline at half its strength, stopped at the shelf's line. Its strength is carried as the rim's opacity: `tab-rim-mix-chosen` on the chosen tab (`tab-rim-mix-idle` when it is idle or done, whose muted ink needs more of itself), `tab-rim-mix-subtle` on an unchosen one, nothing on an unchosen idle one. A status changing at rest cross-fades it and its glow over `--dur-panel`; a switch carries it on the switch's clock, each tab's strength following how chosen it stands at that frame, so a rim fades in or out, or to its quieter strength, as its tab comes forward or recedes. The chosen rim, top and sides, holds 3:1 in both themes (WCAG non-text contrast). Its top against the shelf: 4.1:1 by day and 5.7:1 by night working, 3.2:1 and 3.8:1 idle. Its sides, at their mid-height against the fills either side: a chosen working tab's edge stands 4.5:1 by day and 5.0:1 by night on the desk (4.4:1 and 5.0:1 on the phone), an idle one 3.5:1 and 3.4:1 (3.6:1 and 3.4:1). Their taper (`tab-rim-taper`, 32px over a 1px stroke, the owner's tuning) eases out from the tip, so the stroke is whole well before mid-height. By day the strength is 0.97 chosen and 0.86 idle or done, and a tab that needs you wears its rim in `tab-rim-attn`, the attention glyph's hue darker, because the glyph's own tone holds only 3.2:1 against the shelf even whole. By night it is 0.78 and 0.54, in the glyph's own tone. An unchosen rim is a status mark, not a boundary (its card's edge is that): at 0.5 by day and by night (the owner's "50pct") its 1px top stands 1.5 to 1.7:1 against the shelf by day and 2.1 to 3.0:1 by night (failed the faintest, needs you the strongest), clearly under the chosen tab's 4.1:1 by day and 5.7:1 by night.

**Rim taper.** A rim never stops on a hard cut (owner: "the rim should taper-progress-fade at the very edges"). At each free end, the tip of a foot on the shelf, the stroke narrows to a point and fades to nothing over its last `tab-rim-taper` (32px, the owner's tuning) along the stroke: on every tab, in both themes, at rest and on every frame of a switch. The fade eases out from the tip (`tab-rim-taper-ease`, 6): at x of the taper's length its ink is 1 - (1 - x)^6, so it is 0 at the tip, rises steeply off it, is 90% whole by a third of the length (10.2px of 32) and whole at its end, a short faint tail left at the very tip. The stroke narrows on the same curve, to a point at the tip from full width where the fade is 90% whole. On a plain foot the fade runs straight up the outline (a gradient mask, the curve as its stops) and the stroke narrows in from full width there (or at the foot's top, where that is lower), its inner edge leaving the tip square across the stroke, an ease-out. On the flared foot the fade runs round the flare's quarter turn (a conic mask about its centre) and on up the run where the taper is longer than the flare, and the stroke keeps its width round the flare until that point (at most 60° up the turn, past which the curve would swell wider than the stroke), then narrows along a curve that leaves the flare's circle and lands flat on the shelf, the widest the stroke can stand between the flare and the shelf. The fade stops short of the shoulders whatever its length. All of it is CSS masks and `shape()` paths.

The rim leans with its tab and is drawn in pieces: the top, and each side twice, its shoulder and run standing on the plain foot and on the flared one, each one shape from shoulder to tip so no line shows where a run meets its foot. Each tab's rim stands at its tab's own rank in the stack, over its own card and under every tab nearer the choice, as one folder over another: where two tabs overlap, the front tab's card covers the side of the one behind, whose top and shoulder run on under it and meet the front's outline at its edge, so a corner is one line, with no stub and no notch. Where the tab in front wears no rim, the seam has no stroke: the front card's edge is the edge, and the tab behind draws none there, its side under that card. On a switch that changes which of two tabs is in front, the edge where they meet glides between their two edges over the whole clock: the tab in front at that frame draws it there, its card standing in with it by up to the overlap less its stroke, and the tab behind stands at its own edge, under the front's card, with no foot on that side: its top and shoulder still run on under the card to the corner, but on a switch the front card's foot row (a compositor layer's edge) is half clear, and a foot under it showed through on the shelf as a stub. The front changes at half way, at equal depth (the stack's rule), where the two stand in on the same pixels. A gliding side's ink leans toward its neighbour's by how far it has come, and that side alone, its shoulder, run and foot, leans its strength toward the weaker of the two rims' by half way, so at the handover the two are the same stroke in the same ink at the same strength and nothing moves (transitions.dev, banner stacking: "the swap happens at identity"); against a neighbour with no rim the gliding side fades out, its glow with it. The rest of the rim, its top and its far side, keeps its tab's own strength on every frame, moving one way on the switch's clock from its resting strength to the next: a rim never blinks out on a switch, not even on a jump past a tab with no rim, where the outgoing rim fades from chosen to its resting strength and the incoming one fades up while only their seams beside the rimless tab dip. So a seam is one stroke where its front tab wears a rim and none where it wears none, in every frame, and never jumps from one edge to the other or in strength; a side never draws from under a neighbour. The feet are the sheet's: on every frame of the switch's clock (`--dur-settle` on `--ease-crossfade`) a tab's flared side shows exactly as much as its sheet does, and its plain side the rest, so a tab's body, its feet and its rim move as one shape and the curves never follow the body a half behind (owner: the tab animation "animates the tabs body then the curve on the sides separately"). What keeps a flare from showing from under a neighbour is the stack, not a timing: a side under its neighbour's card draws no foot, as the sheet's own foot there lies under that card, and the two trade at half way with the front. The two always sum to one and add as light (`plus-lighter`), so where they run on the same pixels the stroke holds its strength. The flared foot's taper runs round its quarter turn from the tip and on up the run as one fade, with no line where the turn meets the run, upright or leaning. A swipe draws all of it from its fraction. The iOS app draws the same feet: each side's body (shoulder, run and plain foot) with a fade that adds the two feet's tapers by how chosen the tab stands, and the flared foot round the sheet's own flare at the sheet's opacity, its glow likewise. Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency draw a solid 1px rim with no glow on every tab that wears one. The desk keeps the status glyph before the title as well. The phone's row draws no glyph, so there the rim is the status (and an unchosen idle tab shows none).

## Components

The kit lives in `apps/dashboard/src/lib/components/ui`. Shared recipes are the `kit-*` classes in `app.css`.

### Buttons
Tactile and quiet: a hairline-edged raised block that dips under the hand.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px). Default 36px tall with 14px padding, an 8px gap and 16px icons. `sm` is 30px at the label size with 11px padding. `xs` is 24px. `lg` is 44px. Icon sizes match (36, 30, 24, 44).
- **Outline (default kit look):** raised surface, 1px `border-button-outline` edge (3:1), strong ink, hover to `surface-hover`.
- **Primary:** the coral `action-surface` gradient over `action-solid`, with no border and `on-action` text; hover runs `action-surface-hover` over `action-hover`.
- **Secondary:** recess surface with a hairline edge.
- **Ghost:** transparent until hovered or expanded.
- **Destructive:** transparent with an `error-9` edge and `error-11` text, hovering to `error-3`.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity; the colour pair is kept.
- **Pending:** a button running work keeps its focus and its place but swallows presses. Its icon slot cross-fades from icon to spinner to a drawn check, the label morphs letter by letter to its pending wording ("Saving…"), and the width follows over 180ms on ease-out. On success the check holds for 1.2s. A button with no rest icon opens its slot from zero width as the work starts.
- **Motion:** hover and colour changes take 120ms; the press is below.

**The Press Rule.** The hand feels a control before release, in one of two ways chosen by size. A compact standalone control (a button, an icon button, a chip, a switch, about 44px tall at most) scales to 0.97 over 160ms on ease-out. Anything wide, large or grouped (rows, cards, card heads, tabs in a strip, segments) tints to `surface-fill` and moves nothing, because a 0.97 scale pulls a 300px row's edge in by 4.5px. A text link dims to 72% opacity. The scale only runs with motion allowed; the tint always runs. The tint outranks a control's own background by specificity (an id's weight through `:is()`), never by `!important`, which keeps its fade off the compositor.

### Inputs and fields
- **Style:** 36px tall, 10px radius, raised surface, 1px control edge, the extra-small shadow, body text, `ink-strong` text, muted placeholder, coral caret (`brand-solid`).
- **Focus:** the focus ring drawn over the field's own border (inset offset), so the border becomes the ring and nothing stacks on it.

A parent with a count shows it on the tile in place of the face ("99" from 100 up) and stands on a deck: up to three copies of the tile behind it, each 11% smaller and 1.25px lower than the one in front (`deck-shrink`, `deck-step`), stepping darker. The mark is the switch that opens and folds its rows: on a fine pointer's hover, and while open, what the tile shows gives way to Solar's duotone `alt-arrow-right` (opacity 0 to 1, scale 0.25 to 1, blur 4px to 0, both ways, `--dur-control` on ease-out), turned down while open, and the tile presses to 0.97. A session's mark takes the press itself, inside the row's link; a project's and the older row's rows are buttons, so the row is the switch and the mark only draws the morph. A project's count is its running sessions, a status of its own and not the rows under it, so it stays on the tile while the project is open and the chevron shows only under a pointer. Open, the deck is away: its cards are the rows under it, and the first three leave from it and come back to it.
- **Session row:** one row for a session in every list that names sessions (`SessionRow.svelte`): the rail's project tree, the home's Working and Finished, Recent and the drawer. Its mark, then its title with the status word read out before it, then its age at the trailing edge; the row is the session menu's trigger, can be dragged to split, tints on press, and at rest is never a stacking context. Two sizes: two lines in the home lists (the project and what it is doing under the title, 32px at a desk and 44px under a finger), and `compact` under a project in the rail (one line, `row-compact-h` 28px, `row-compact-gap` 6px, the age in a 3ch column). Peek and Archive rise at the row's end where a list offers them. Peek is in the wide layout only, desktop and iPad, not phones (the drawer layout under 900px draws no Peek): with a fine pointer it rises on hover or focus, Archive a step left of it, and the words yield their room; on an iPad's touch screen it always shows, in the row's flow at its end, with 44px of touch area, its glyph at the touch size (`icon-lg`) in `ink-muted`, and no tooltip (a press would leave one standing over the sheet it opened), and Archive is a swipe. The rail's tree offers no Peek. A list owns its container, order and nesting; nothing inside the row.
- **Working indicator:** a working session's tile echoes. A copy of the tile grows from it to 1.45 and fades from 45% over one 1.2s loop on ease-out, transform and opacity only, seen only outside the tile and its deck; the tile and its deck stay still. It is under them by shape, not by order: the mark draws it last, in a box as large as it grows, cut to the outside of the tile and of each card's band. A layer that moves takes whatever is drawn after it onto layers too, so nothing is drawn after the echo. The working rows of one list beat top to bottom a third of a loop apart, on the clock, so a row that joins is already in step (`motion/echo`). Between beats the copy waits at the tile's own size under the cut, never at opacity 0 (a wholly transparent layer is not drawn, and was presented un-painted on the frame its beat began), and it is away while the tree it is in opens. It echoes three loops from when the row took the status, then rests as reduced motion draws it: a loop with nothing new to say spends a frame at every refresh, and a test harness takes an app with an animation running as never idle. A new status echoes afresh; the same status again does not. With reduced motion nothing echoes: a still status dot in the live ink says it.
- **Status dot:** the one status shape on a tree mark. A 6px circle (`status-dot-size`) centred on the tile's top-right corner, so it stands 3px out of the tile up and right, over the tile, its count or chevron and its deck, in the status glyph ink (`status-attn-glyph`, `status-fail-glyph`, `status-live-glyph`). The tile is cut away 1.5px round it (`status-dot-cut`), a hole in the tile's mask, so the surface the row stands on shows round the dot on any tile hue and over the hover and selected washes, in both themes. Failed: a still dot. Needs you: the dot echoes with the working indicator's own keyframes, curve and loop, in the list's beat order with its working rows, three loops and then still. Working: no dot while the tile echoes; once its echoes have played, and with reduced motion, a still dot. Idle and finished: nothing. There is no ring round a tile. The dot is decoration: the row's name says the status word. A row that is not a session ("N older") leads with a duotone glyph in `ink-muted` in the same 18px slot, on the tiles' axis, with no tile under it and no arrowhead.
- **Icon tile:** a raised 26px square at 8px radius with the tile shadow, holding a 16px duotone glyph in its section or machine hue. Chosen, it inverts to an `ink-solid` fill with an `on-ink` glyph.
- **Machine row:** tile, name in label, a meta line led by a 6px presence dot (green online, orange away, neutral-8 off).

### Icons
**The Solar Duotone Rule.** Every product icon is Solar's bold-duotone cut, imported through `$lib/icons.ts`: one face for the whole product. The duotone's second layer is the same `currentColor` at 50% opacity. A status glyph in an alert row (the joined row in Connect a machine) is the row's signal, so its second layer takes `glyph-duo-status-opacity` (0.81), which holds both tones at 3:1 on the recess in both schemes. Chevrons and arrows (`alt-arrow-*`, `arrow-*`) use Solar's linear cut because they are glyphs, not marks. Close, Minus, Plus and Tick are local glyphs drawn to Solar's 24px grid with a 1.5 linear stroke and round caps, because Solar has no bare version.
- **Sizes:** 12px beside meta text, 16px in controls and rows (the default for any unsized icon), 20px in nav and headers.

### Focus and hit areas
**The One Ring Rule.** Every focusable element shows one ring on keyboard focus only: a 2px solid outline in `focus-ring`, 2px outside the element. Text fields, list rows, tabs, options and anything flush against a container or inside a clipping scroller carry the ring inset on their own border box instead, so it never stacks on a border or gets cut. The ring's colour is set at rest, so focus only switches it on. A scroller brings a focused control into view with room for its ring. Pointer focus shows nothing. The one exception is the composer grown for the recall wheel or a queued edit: its shape keeps its border and draws no ring (The grown shape).

**The 44 Touch Rule.** On a coarse pointer every control's tappable area reaches 44×44 through a transparent `::after` (`.touch-hit`, WCAG 2.5.8), while its drawn size and the layout stay the same. Where neighbours sit closer, `--hit-gap-x` and `--hit-gap-y` stop each area at the midpoint so areas never overlap; clusters open their gap on touch (8 to 14px for a row's controls, to 16px in the top bar). A scroller that would clip the areas pads into an equal negative margin. Menu items and nav rows are 44px tall on touch.

**The 24 Pointer Rule.** On a fine pointer, a control drawn smaller than 24×24 reaches 24px through `.pointer-hit`, with the same midpoint stops. `--hit-scale` keeps the area at 24px on screen inside a zoomed canvas.

**Hover only where it exists.** Hover styles sit under `(hover: hover)` or a fine pointer, so a tap never leaves a stuck hover. The pointer-driven ghost never appears on touch.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, radius, type role, spacing step, curve and duration from the tokens in `design/tokens/cawco.tokens.json`. Add a token there and run `bun run tokens` before adding a one-off value.
- **Do** keep the action coral with its top-highlight gradient, and spend other hue only on status, identity and threshold data.
- **Do** check every change in both schemes; neither is the default.
- **Do** measure white on any new coral fill to 4.5:1, top gradient stop included.
- **Do** recess the frame and raise the content; the stat well is the only card-in-card.
- **Do** say status with a glyph or a word before hue, and draw idle as no chip at all.
- **Do** set every changing figure in tabular numerals (`.num`), and keep weights at 400 and 500.
- **Do** use Solar bold-duotone for every product icon, linear only for chevrons and arrows, at 12, 16 or 20px.
- **Do** make a compact control scale to 0.97 on press and a wide or grouped one tint to `surface-fill`.
- **Do** give every touch control a 44×44 area and every small pointer target a 24×24 area without changing the drawn size.
- **Do** enter on ease-out over 260 to 280ms and leave over 160ms; move things already on screen on ease-in-out.
- **Do** carry shared objects across a change, and reflow lists rather than letting rows jump.
- **Do** put every travelling motion under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` (or `motionOk` in script), and keep fades for reduced motion.
- **Do** show a skeleton at the real layout's size while data loads, and the empty state only once the data is known to be empty.
- **Do** keep sound off until the reader turns it on, play every cue at the subtle emphasis for a gesture the reader made, and give touch only to a finger (The Quiet Feel Rule).
- **Do** keep Approve and Deny as equal peers at opposite ends of their row, with the standing grant set apart below.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use coral for a status, crimson or spark for an action, or introduce indigo anywhere as a brand or accent hue.
- **Don't** use a hue for decoration, or use crimson, spark yellow, olive or blue for anything but status.
- **Don't** set text on `brand-solid`, turn a tooltip or ink tile coral, or put spark, paper or crow ink in product chrome.
- **Don't** set product text in Fredoka, or code in anything but JetBrains Mono with ligatures off.
- **Don't** write a token value in `app.css` or a component, or edit the generated `tokens.css`.
- **Don't** set any text above weight 500, or pick a size without a role.
- **Don't** use `rgba(0,0,0,·)` shadows; shadows come from the warm ink tints.
- **Don't** use an ease-in curve, or let an exit run longer than its entrance.
- **Don't** keep an arrived surface moving: no pulse or attention loop on anything that is not live work.
- **Don't** play a sound or a tap to announce something, or make one the only sign that something happened.
- **Don't** draw a focus indicator as a box-shadow, a border-colour change or a background, and don't stack a ring on a border.
- **Don't** truncate on the control or link itself, which clips its hit area.
- **Don't** render a text field under 16px on a phone or a coarse pointer.
- **Don't** swap Solar for another icon set or for SF Symbols, or drop the duotone layer.
- **Don't** use pill-shaped buttons; full rounding is for state pills, switches and dots.
- **Don't** put a third control between Approve and Deny, or make one more salient than the other.
- **Don't** show an empty state while data is loading, or claim zero while the hub connection is not live.
- **Don't** use a `group-has-*` utility anywhere in the dashboard: one such rule in the sheet makes every element put into the page restyle the whole rail, 20ms and more, whether its group is on the page or not. Style from a state the component sets on itself or its group (`group-data-*`), or from a sibling. `grep -rn "group-has-" apps/dashboard/src` finds none.
