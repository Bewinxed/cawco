# Design System: CawCo

**The token source is `design/tokens/cawco.tokens.json`** (W3C DTCG). It is the one place a visual value is defined. `bun run tokens` in `apps/dashboard` builds it with Style Dictionary into `apps/dashboard/src/lib/tokens/tokens.css`. That is a `@layer theme` block where every colour that differs by scheme is `light-dark(light, dark)`. `app.css` imports it and defines no token of its own; its `@theme inline` blocks only bridge tokens to Tailwind utilities. The same command, from the same Style Dictionary config (`apps/dashboard/scripts/build-tokens.ts`, its Apple platform in `build-tokens-apple.ts`), writes the native apps' tokens into `apps/apple/Packages/CawCoKit/Sources/CawCoDesign/`: `Resources/Tokens.xcassets`, one colour set per colour token with Any and Dark appearances (Display P3, gamut-mapped), and `Generated/Tokens.swift`, with `Palette` colour accessors and `TypeScale`, `Space`, `Radius`, `Size`, `Effect` and `Motion` constants (points, seconds, `Font.Weight`, `UnitCurve`, `TypeRole`). Shadows, gradients and transitions stay web-only. A token's `$value` is its light (or only) value, and `$extensions["dev.cawco"]` carries `dark`, `coarse` (pointer: coarse) and `compact` (coarse or under 640px) variants. To change a value, edit the JSON and rebuild; never edit `tokens.css` or add a literal to a component.

The frontmatter above restates the shipped colours as the browser computes them, in both schemes, for tools that read DESIGN.md. Where it and the JSON disagree, the JSON wins.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Ledger, re-skinned for Caw&Co"**

CawCo is a board an operator glances at between other work. It answers one question: does anything need me right now? The design treats that glance like reading a departure board. Rows hold still, numbers line up, and a quiet row has to mean a healthy row. The copy is a calm operator's log, not a personality brand (`WORDS.md`).

The direction (Caw&Co "3b · Coral action") keeps the shipped ledger's density, metrics, spacing, radii, motion and Solar icons, and re-skins its colour and type. The surface is warm paper by day (neutral hue ~85) and roost-dark at night (hue ~70). Chroma stays low in all chrome. Colour is spent on five things only: the coral action (where you act), status, identity marks, the coral focus ring, and data that crosses a threshold. Depth comes from a recessed frame holding raised content, not from heavy shadows. The density is set by the operator's job: 44px rows, a 32px header band, 90px stat tiles and the 14px body size.

Neither scheme is the original. The theme class on `<html>` (resolved from the reader's choice or the OS by `theme.svelte.ts`) sets `color-scheme`, and every colour token picks its side through `light-dark()`. A design is drawn and checked in both, never light-first or dark-first.

Motion is continuity, never decoration. Something that exists on both sides of a change travels to its new place rather than vanishing and reappearing, and a list that changes reflows rather than jumps. Nothing moves after it has landed unless it is live work, and live work breathes slowly. The code rejects: ease-in curves, `rgba(0,0,0,…)` shadows, weights above 500, indigo, and hue used for decoration.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A coral action on warm paper; colour elsewhere only where it carries meaning.
- The frame recesses; content sits raised on it.
- Figtree for the product, JetBrains Mono for code, paths and IDs, Fredoka 600 for brand display only. Product weights 400 and 500 only.
- A spacing ladder measured off the reference comps (4, 7, 11, 14, 18, 21, 25, 32), not a 4px grid.
- Solar bold-duotone icons throughout, linear only for chevrons and arrows.
- Motion that keeps one object on screen through every change, honours reduced motion everywhere, and exits faster than it enters.

### Motion grammar

All motion stands on three curves and a short duration scale (values in the sidecar's `extensions.motion`).

- **Ease-out** (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`) carries entrances, exits and any change in place.
- **Ease-in-out** (`cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`) carries things already on screen moving from one place to another: rows sliding, a thumb gliding between segments, a height tween in the transcript.
- **Drawer** (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`) carries floating surfaces, sheets, page swaps, shared-element flights and container size changes.

**The No Ease-In Rule.** No ease-in curve exists. Anything that starts slow feels like the interface hesitating.

**The Fast Exit Rule.** Exits are shorter than entrances. A popover opens over 260ms and closes over 160ms. A dialog opens over 280ms and closes over 160ms. A toast rises over 280ms and leaves over 160ms.

**The One Object Rule.** What is on both sides of a change travels; it never vanishes on one side and appears on the other (`motion/share.svelte.ts`). A sidebar row becomes the tab it opens, a section row's title flies into the editor header, a sent message flies from the composer into the transcript row it became. A flight runs 280ms on the drawer curve, as a transform (FLIP) or a clip that opens from the source's box. The source is hidden while its copy flies, so the screen never shows two.

**The Nothing Jumps Rule.** When a list changes, rows that stay slide to their new places, rows that leave close while fading, and rows that arrive are uncovered top to bottom while what follows makes room (`motion/rows.svelte.ts`). Movement takes 280ms on ease-in-out, arrivals fade over 260ms, departures fade over 160ms, all started on the same frame. A move that happens entirely off screen jumps instead of sliding, so nothing drifts into view behind the reader's scroll.

**The Interruptible Rule.** Every transition turns back from where it is drawn. Popovers transition on `data-state` rather than playing keyframes, folds animate from the drawn height, and a segmented thumb caught mid-glide restarts from where it stands.

**Springs.** Surfaces released by a finger settle on one spring (`motion/spring.ts`): perceptual duration 0.4s, bounce 0, mass 1, stiffness `(2π / 0.4)²` ≈ 246.7, damping `4π / 0.4` ≈ 31.4 (critically damped). The spring is integrated once at release at 120Hz and handed to the compositor as keyframes about 8ms apart, carrying the finger's velocity. A tab track scrolled with a mouse wheel glides on a second, critically damped spring with a 0.3s response (stiffness `(2π / 0.3)²` ≈ 438.6, damping `4π / 0.3` ≈ 41.9).

**Live work breathes.** Anything still running pulses its opacity on `--breath` (2s, ease-in-out): the catch-up dot, a subagent's beat, the in-flight tool glyph, the Working status glyph. Nothing that has finished moves.

**The One Loop Rule.** Anything that turns or blinks while something is pending runs one ambient loop, `--dur-loop` (1.2s): a spinner, the task ring's arc, the supervisor's orbit, the skeleton's sweep, and Tailwind's `animate-spin`, `animate-pulse` and `animate-ping`, which the theme points at the token. A rotation turns linearly; a pulse runs ease-in-out and a ping ease-out. Longer rhythms are multiples of the breath, never new numbers: the thinking indicator's words change every two breaths. Tailwind's default transition timing (`transition-colors` and its siblings) is `--dur-control` on ease-out.

**The Reduced Motion Rule.** Motion is opt-in. Every travelling transition and keyframe sits under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and JavaScript motion asks the same question through `motionOk` (`motion/curves.svelte.ts`). With reduced motion, opacity and colour fades still run, pages cross-fade in place over 120ms, heights land at once, folds fade instead of opening, list changes only fade, flights do not fly, and loops stop (an indeterminate spinner keeps turning). Reduced motion means stillness. It does not mean an instant swap.

**Pages.** The shell keys its page on the route (`motion/route.svelte.ts`). Between two sidebar spokes the page moves 8% vertically, in the order the sidebar runs, over 120ms. Deeper or shallower moves nudge 8% horizontally. Browser back and forward follow the history step and flip in right-to-left layouts. On a phone, Configure behaves as a stack: a deeper page pushes in from the inline end over 280ms while the page it leaves falls back 30% and dims to 0.6, and back reverses it over 260ms. On a wide screen, Configure sections rise or drop 4% over 140ms while the rail holds still. Everything runs on the drawer curve, and the leaving page is inert.

**Theme switch.** The new theme is drawn at once under a wash of the old background, which fades off over 200ms on ease-out (`theme.svelte.ts`, `.theme-wash`). Element transitions are switched off for the flip. With reduced motion the theme flips with no wash.

### Named Rules
**The Reserved Hue Rule.** Crimson, spark yellow, olive and blue mean status, and status only. Progress rings, identity and decoration take `currentColor` or an identity hue. A finished task ring is the one moment a decoration earns a state colour, because by then it is a result (`TaskRing.svelte`).

**The Coral Is Where You Act Rule.** Coral marks the action, the selected nav row, the focus ring, checked controls, the chosen option and the selection tint. It is never a status: failed is crimson and needs-you is spark, so coral never reads as an alarm and an alarm never reads as a button.

**The White On Coral Rule.** Text on coral sits on `action-solid` or `chip-chosen-bg`, the measured fills, never on `brand-solid`. A new coral fill under text is measured to 4.5:1, top gradient stop included, before it ships.

**The Ink Stays Ink Rule.** Tooltips and inverted tiles are `ink-solid` with `on-ink`. They are never coral.

**The Idle Has No Fill Rule.** An idle session carries no chip. A quiet row means a healthy row, so rest is drawn as nothing.

**The No Indigo Rule.** Indigo is never a brand, action or accent hue. Identity hues span the wheel for telling things apart and are never promoted to a brand role.

**The Glyph, Word, Hue Rule.** Status is never colour alone. The session status reads glyph, then word, then hue (a Solar duotone glyph, then "Working", "Needs you", "Failed", "Idle", "Sleeping", "Stopped" or "Unreachable"). ## Typography

**UI font:** Figtree Variable (`@fontsource-variable/figtree`, with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif), at 400 and 500 only
**Mono font:** JetBrains Mono Variable (`@fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono`, with ui-monospace, SF Mono, monospace), ligatures off
**Display font:** Fredoka 600 (`@fontsource/fredoka`), brand display only: the wordmark, marketing, a brand moment in an empty state. Never product chrome.

**Character:** Figtree has round forms that sit near the Caw&Co wordmark and stays crisp at 12 to 13px in dense rows. JetBrains Mono has a tall x-height and a clear 0/O and 1/l/I in diffs. It sets large, so code runs at 12px (`--text-code`). Ligatures are off for every code, path and ID run (`font-variant-ligatures: none`). Figtree's and JetBrains Mono's latin variable faces are inlined into the stylesheet at build time (`vite.config.ts` INLINE_FACES), and `app.html` starts their decode before the body is parsed, so the first paint never swaps fonts.

### Hierarchy
- **Meta** (400, 12px, 1.35): timestamps, counts, units, hints, table data on the board, toast meta.
- **Label** (500, 13px, 1.3): names, group headings, badges, chips, small buttons, menu items, segment labels.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.45): sentences, descriptions, alert text, prose. Running text measures 60 to 72ch.
- **Button** (500, 14px, 1, −0.01em): the default 36px button.
- **Prose** (400, 14px, 1.55): transcript prose and rendered markdown.
- **Title** (500, 18→20px, 1.25, −0.01em): page titles, dialog titles, empty-state titles. It scales with the viewport between 390px and 1024px.
- **KPI** (500, 20→24px, 1, tabular): a stat tile's figure. It scales over the same range and drops to the title size under 900px.
- **Column head** (500, 13px, uppercase, 0.06em tracking): a ledger's header band.
- **Code** (JetBrains Mono 400, 12px, 1.6, ligatures off): code, commands, paths, tool names, keycaps.
- **Display** (Fredoka 600, 40px): brand only, the one place weight goes above 500.

The three dense roles (meta, label, body) keep one size at every width because they are already the smallest that reads. Rendered markdown restates its scale from these roles: h1 and h2 take the title size at 1.2 leading and −0.008em, h3 to h6 take the body size, and `strong`, `th`, `dt` and `summary` take 500.

### Named Rules
**The 500 Ceiling Rule.** No product text goes above 500; Fredoka's 600 is brand display only. Emphasis is a weight step from 400 to 500 or an ink step, never bold. The prose plugin's 600 to 800 weights are overridden back to 500.

**The Role, Not Size Rule.** Text takes a role by what it does. Names, group headings, column heads and badges are label. Sentences and descriptions are body. Timestamps, counts, units and hints are meta. Nobody picks a size and finds a role for it.

**The Tabular Figure Rule.** A figure that changes (a count, a price, an age, a percentage) carries `.num` and is set in tabular numerals, so a ticking value never moves its neighbours. Tables get tabular numerals by default. The KPI role always carries `.num`, because a `font` shorthand resets `font-variant-numeric`.

**The Truncate Inside Rule.** Text truncates with an ellipsis in an inner span, never on the control or link itself, so the hit area is never clipped. In a row, the name gives way first and the meta line keeps one line.

**The 16px Field Rule.** On a phone or a coarse pointer every text field renders at 16px (an `!important` in the base layer), because iOS Safari zooms into any field under 16px.

**Balanced wrapping.** Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. Paragraphs and list items use `pretty`.

## Layout

The shell is a resizable rail (228px by default, the reader's own width wins), a 60px top bar with a 1px hairline, and a page on the recessed field. A page pads 25px on the inline start and 21px on the end, and the groups of a page (stat row, attention queue, cards) stand 18px apart. A page head is 96px from the bar to the first group, with the title over its one-line description and the actions centred on the pair.

**Spacing ladder.** 4, 7, 11, 14, 18, 21, 25 and 32px, measured off the reference comps. It is deliberately not a 4px ladder.

**Density.** Management surfaces run the comps' density: a 44px row pitch, a 32px header band, a 55px toolbar zone (with 32px toolbar controls on the fleet board only) and 90px stat tiles. The transcript runs a compact scale from the same tokens. Under a coarse pointer, compact relaxes to the touch floor.

**Fleet board.** Four stat tiles in a grid (two columns under 900px), the attention queue, any machines the board has something to say about, then the sessions ledger. The ledger uses a fixed table layout: the name column takes what the fixed columns leave (machine 140, harness 110, turns 64, context 76, last active 132, state 124, actions 124) and truncates. Under 640px each row becomes a two-line card: the title across the row, then machine · state · age, with the actions at the end.

**Transcript columns.** Every transcript row lays out on four edges measured from its own inline start: a 2px rail at 7px, a 16px glyph cell 11px past the rail, and the text column 7px past the cell. A nested transcript (a delegate's or a subagent's) starts at its parent's text column and repeats the edges. Under 900px the rail sits at 0. The transcript pads 25px / 21px inline (18px under 900px) and clears the floating composer at its foot.

**Composer column.** At most 720px wide, centred, docked 14px above the bottom plus the safe area. Under 900px it goes full width with 11px margins.

**Breakpoints.** 640px (phone: board rows become cards, toasts move to the top, the new-session dialog becomes a bottom sheet, popovers widen to the viewport less 24px), 900px (the rail becomes a burger sheet, the transcript rail moves to 0, the composer goes full width, stat tiles go to two columns), and 1024px (where the fluid title and KPI sizes reach their maximum). A coarse pointer counts as narrow for nav rows (44px) and for hosting the tab strip.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal first and shadowed second. The frame (app field, top bar sheet, dialog tray, header band, segmented groove) recesses. Content (cards, rows, menus, the dialog body, a segmented thumb) sits raised on it. Shadows are warm ink tints: by day the ink at low alpha, at night the deepest surface at high alpha plus a 1px inner ring of ink (`night-ring`, transparent by day, ink at 7% at night). Every shadow token adapts through `light-dark()` on its colours; none is keyed on the theme class.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Tile** (`0 1px 2px` ink at 7% by day / black at 30% at night, plus a 1px ring of ink at 3.5% / 6%): cards, machine tiles, icon tiles, report cards.
- **Hairline** (`0 3px 7px -2px` shadow-tint-2): a drop under a tile without a ring.
- **Stat** (the hairline drop plus the night ring): the stat tile and the permission card.
- **Raised** (`0 1px 3px` and `0 1px 1px` shadow-tint, plus the night ring): a thumb in a groove. At night the ring makes it read as a hairline-edged lift.
- **Overlay** (`0 18px 48px` shadow-tint-3, `0 2px 6px` shadow-tint, plus the night ring): popovers, menus, the dialog tray, toasts, floating tags. The modal shadow is the same value.
- **Drawer** (`0 12px 40px` shadow-tint-3): sheets.
- **Action ring** (`action-ring`: a white top hairline at 18% and a coral-tinted drop) and **code ring** (`code-ring`: an inner vermillion hairline and glow) are in the token source for the action and code blocks.

The Tailwind ladder has four steps, `shadow-xs`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg` and `shadow-xl` (Tailwind's own scale is cleared, so no other step exists). md, lg and xl carry the night ring.

### Materials
- **Scrim**: the page's own background at 72% with a 2px blur, behind dialogs and sheets.
- **Panel material**: the raised surface at 72%, with a 28px blur and 1.6 saturation, for chrome that floats over content. It turns opaque under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`.
- **Composer glass**: the panel material at the base blur (`--material-blur`, 20px, and 1.6 saturation), so the transcript shows faintly under the field; opaque raised surface under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`.

### Named Rules
**The Frame Recesses Rule.** The frame recesses and content never does. The one sanctioned card-in-card is the stat tile's recessed well, which the reference plan calls "a signature move of the design".

**The Warm Shadow Rule.** Shadows are warm ink taken from the neutral ramp, never `rgba(0,0,0,·)`. The tile's night drop is the one black, at 30%, under a ring of ink.

**The Night Ring Rule.** At night a raised surface is told apart from its frame by a 1px inner ring of ink as well as its drop. The ring is part of the shadow token, so a component that takes the token gets it in both schemes without a class.

## Shapes

Corners are soft and graded by role: 2px (`--radius-hair`) for marks too small to round further (chart swatches, a chart tooltip's key, the tooltip's arrow), 5px for marks and tiles, 8px for items inside a surface, 10px for controls and buttons, 12px for cards and menus, and 18px for the dialog tray. The stat well uses 7px. A fully round shape is kept for state pills on the board, the compacting note, switches and status dots; buttons are never pills.

**The Concentric Rule.** A surface nested inside another takes the outer radius less the inset. The dialog tray (18px, 6px padding) holds a 12px body. The composer shell (12px, 7px inset) holds 5px controls, and the autopilot's halo 3px outside its control is drawn at 8px. A subagent's well (8px, 4px padding) holds a 5px report.

**The Measured Exception Rule.** The stat tile keeps the comp's measured radii (a 10px card around a 7px well, 7px in), even though a concentric well would be 3px. The assistant panel keeps its measured 16px shell (`--radius-panel`, JOURNEY.md §Assistant), and the smallest marks keep a 2px hair (`--radius-hair`) rather than the 5px that would turn them into dots. Each is a token, never a literal.

**Borders are graded.** `border-hairline` separates rows and regions. `border-control` draws a control's edge. At night the generic border becomes ink at 8% (a seam) and the input edge becomes ink at 14%.

**Folder tabs.** A chosen folder tab is a sheet in the surface below it, with rounded shoulders and a foot that flares outward into the page (a CSS `shape()` path at the tab radius). Unchosen tabs stand on the shelf in `surface-recess-deep` with rounded tops.

**Focus outline.** Focus is an outline, never a box-shadow, so it follows each element's own radius and survives forced colours.

## Components

The kit lives in `apps/dashboard/src/lib/components/ui`. Shared recipes are the `kit-*` classes in `app.css`.

### Buttons
Tactile and quiet: a hairline-edged raised block that dips under the hand.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px). Default 36px tall with 14px padding, an 8px gap and 16px icons. `sm` is 30px at the label size with 11px padding. `xs` is 24px. `lg` is 44px. Icon sizes match (36, 30, 24, 44).
- **Outline (default kit look):** raised surface, 1px control edge, strong ink, hover to `surface-hover`.
- **Primary:** the coral `action-surface` gradient over `action-solid`, with no border and `on-action` text; hover runs `action-surface-hover` over `action-hover`.
- **Secondary:** recess surface with a hairline edge.
- **Ghost:** transparent until hovered or expanded.
- **Destructive:** transparent with an `error-9` edge and `error-11` text, hovering to `error-3`.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity; the colour pair is kept.
- **Pending:** a button running work keeps its focus and its place but swallows presses. Its icon slot cross-fades from icon to spinner to a drawn check, the label morphs letter by letter to its pending wording ("Saving…"), and the width follows over 180ms on ease-out. On success the check holds for 1.2s. A button with no rest icon opens its slot from zero width as the work starts.
- **Motion:** hover and colour changes take 120ms; the press is below.

**The Press Rule.** The hand feels a control before release, in one of two ways chosen by size. A compact standalone control (a button, an icon button, a chip, a switch, about 44px tall at most) scales to 0.97 over 160ms on ease-out. Anything wide, large or grouped (rows, cards, card heads, tabs in a strip, segments) tints to `surface-fill` and moves nothing, because a 0.97 scale pulls a 300px row's edge in by 4.5px. A text link dims to 72% opacity. The scale only runs with motion allowed; the tint always runs.

### Inputs and fields
- **Style:** 36px tall, 10px radius, raised surface, 1px control edge, the extra-small shadow, body text, `ink-strong` text, muted placeholder, coral caret (`brand-solid`).
- **Focus:** the focus ring drawn over the field's own border (inset offset), so the border becomes the ring and nothing stacks on it.
- **Error / disabled:** the edge turns destructive; disabled fades to 50%.
- **Textarea:** grows a line at a time, measured on a hidden twin and set in pixels so a 120ms height transition carries each new line. It scrolls past its ceiling.
- **Field shell:** a frame around a borderless editor (the composer, a search with an icon) is the field. Its border takes the ring while the text inside has focus.
- **Select trigger:** the input's look with a sort-vertical glyph; it scales on press, or tints when stretched wide.

### Cards and the stat tile
- **Card:** 12px radius, raised surface, the tile shadow, 18px vertical rhythm (12px for `sm`), no border.
- **Stat tile:** a 10px card, 90px tall, holding a recessed well 7px in: recess surface, 1px hairline, 7px radius, 10px padding. The label (label role, muted) sits over the value (KPI role, `ink-stat`, tabular), with its unit in meta on the same baseline. A new value morphs digit by digit over 180ms on ease-out, and a unit pops in from 0.92.
- **Needs-you tile:** a real button wrapping the tile. Its hover, chosen (`status-attn-bg` well with attn ink) and pressed fills paint the well, not the card. Pressing it filters the board to the sessions it counts.

### Badges and state chips
- **Badge:** 20px, 5px radius, label role, recess surface, no border, 12px icons. The `live`, `attn`, `done` and `fail` variants take their status pairs.
- **Board state pill:** fully round, 24px tall, 10px padding, label weight, status tint with no glyph. Idle drops the tint and the padding. When state changes the tint turns over 280ms on ease-out and the word morphs over 180ms, with the width following.

### Navigation, menus and floating surfaces
- **Popover, dropdown, select, context menu, toast** (`kit-pop`): 12px radius, 1px control edge, overlay shadow, 6px padding, raised surface. A popover whose content changes size tweens to the new height over 180ms on the drawer curve.
- **Motion (origin-aware):** opens from its trigger's side over 260ms on the drawer curve, rising 8px and growing from 0.92 as it fades in. It leaves the same way over 160ms. These are transitions on `data-state`, so a close caught mid-open turns back from where it is. With reduced motion only the fade runs.
- **Menu item:** 8px radius, 32px tall (44px on a coarse pointer), label role, 10px padding, 16px icons.
- **Hover ghost and selection pill:** each list has one hover ghost and one selection pill drawn under its rows (`highlight.svelte.ts`). The ghost is filled `surface-ghost`: by day one step past `surface-hover`, which is the field's own step and does not show on the rail. It glides to the row the pointer is on (its mark, its switch and the controls beside it count as the row; between rows, the nearest) over 80ms on ease-in-out and fades in place when the pointer leaves. A menu's ghost follows the highlighted item under arrow keys too. The pill glides to a new selection over 120ms on the drawer curve. A row picked under the ghost becomes the pill at once while the old selection fades where it was, so no frame shows both or neither. On a touch screen a pointer-driven ghost never shows.
- **Ticks and dots:** a checkbox tick draws itself along its stroke over 160ms (120ms in a menu). A radio dot grows from 0.6 as it fades in. With reduced motion both only fade.

### Segmented controls and tabs
- **Segmented** (`kit-segmented`, used by Tabs and single toggle groups): a recess-deep groove with a 10px radius and 3px padding holding a lifted thumb (8px radius, raised shadow). Segments are 30px tall with 10px padding, label weight at line-height 1, muted until chosen. The thumb glides with position and size together over 260ms on ease-in-out, from wherever it is drawn. A segment presses with the tint, never a scale.
- **Fluid tabs** (`fluid-tabs`): a track of 4px padding and 28px items (36px control; 32px items on a coarse pointer). The active segment is a raised sheet with the tile shadow that slides over 160ms on ease-out. The focus ring glides between tabs over 80ms. The hover ghost sits one register down at 40% hover. A scrolling track fades whichever edge has more to scroll, and a mouse wheel moves it sideways on the 0.3s glide spring. Arrow keys move and choose.
- **Folder tabs:** the same track standing on `surface-shelf`. A switch wipes the chosen sheet in from the side facing the old tab while the old sheet wipes out toward the new one (mask size, 260ms, drawer curve). A jump past a neighbour slides the chosen sheet across from the tab it left. A swipe gesture drives both sheets frame by frame.

### Tooltip
Solid ink, never coral: `ink-solid` fill and arrow, `on-ink` text, meta role, 8px radius, 6×10px padding, with a 10px rotated square arrow. It fades in and settles from 0.96 over 140ms on ease-out, and leaves the same way.

### Dialog, sheet and drawer
- **Dialog:** a recess tray (18px radius, 6px padding, overlay shadow) holding a raised body (12px radius, 18px padding) for concentric corners, over the blurred scrim. It enters over 280ms on ease-out rising 6px, exits over 160ms, and tweens to a new height over 180ms when its body changes. The close button is a 30px ghost icon button 12px from the corner.
- **Sheet:** a raised side or edge panel with the drawer shadow. It slides its whole size in over 280ms on the drawer curve and leaves over 160ms; with reduced motion it fades over 120ms.
- **Drag to dismiss:** a sheet follows the finger 1:1 along its axis and rubber-bands past rest (35% of travel, at most 25% of its size). Released past 30% of its size, or flicked faster than 0.3px/ms toward its edge, it leaves on the settle spring at the finger's speed while its scrim fades with the share still on screen. Otherwise the spring puts it back. A mouse never drags; it has the close button and Escape.
- **Drawer (vaul):** rises from the bottom with a 100×6 grabber and a raised inner card (12px radius, inset 8px). The phone version of the new-session dialog uses it and leaves over 160ms on ease-out.
- **The Hold-To-Select Rule.** A press that starts inside a text field in a drawer is not captured by the drawer, so iOS hold-to-select and the Paste callout still reach the field. A held press on a context-menu trigger suppresses the platform callout, because our menu answers it.

### Toast
The floating-surface recipe over sonner: 12px radius, overlay shadow, body type, 8px-radius action buttons that scale on press. It enters from `--pop-rise` short of its place on its edge's side over 280ms on ease-out. The stack makes room over 200ms on ease-in-out. A toast exits toward its edge over 160ms. Under a finger it tracks 1:1 and dismisses past 45px or on a flick. The stack stays expanded, so an arrival grows nothing. On a phone toasts sit at the top under the 60px bar, full width less 12px a side, because at the bottom they would land on the composer. Toasts follow an open modal dialog into the top layer.

### Alert
A compact tinted row, never a slab: 10px radius, 10×12px padding, body type, an optional 16px glyph spanning the title and description. Variants: default (recess), destructive, warning, success and info (each its status pair). No border.

### Empty, loading and skeleton states
- **Empty** (`ui/empty`): a 20px Solar duotone mark in muted ink, a title in the title role, one body line in muted ink up to 60ch saying why, and at most one action. It sits on the column the content would have started on, with 21px of block padding.
- **Skeleton** (`ui/skeleton`): a `surface-fill` block at exactly the size of what it stands for, with an 8px radius. One band of the raised surface at 60% crosses it every 1.2s, by transform only; without motion it sits still. Board skeleton rows are 44px (83px on a phone) with a header band in `surface-band`.
- **Spinner:** a 16px ring at 25% with a 25% arc, turning once per 1.2s loop. It is used inside a pending button's slot, as a toast's loading icon, and where a wait is under the skeleton threshold.

**The Claim Of Nothing Rule.** An empty state claims there is nothing to show, so it renders only once the data is known to be empty, never while it is still being read. While data loads, the skeleton stands in at the real layout's size, and the first real read replaces it in one cross-fade (120ms) rather than as rows arriving.

**The Real Wait Rule.** Caw stands in for a wait (loading, reconnecting) only once it has lasted `--dur-wait-grace` (1s; NN/g: no feedback is needed under 1.0s). Until then the area is its plain surface, with no Caw and no line. Once he appears he stays until his enter (his own clip, about 0.8 s) has played, and what arrives waits for that, so he is never cut off mid-entrance. After that what arrives lands at once under its own `--dur-fade` cross-fade and he plays his exit over it. Caw never fades: he appears, leaves and changes status by his clips (assets/mascot/README.md, Contract), except under Reduce Motion, where he fades over `--dur-fade`. Web: SessionSurface's detail area; Apple: `CawWaiting`.

### Status displays
- **Session status:** a 16px Solar duotone glyph (refresh-circle for Working, hand-shake for Needs you, close-circle for Failed, pause-circle for Idle and Stopped, moon-sleep for Sleeping, question-circle for Unreachable), then the word in the label role, tinted live, attn or fail where it applies. A new glyph cross-fades in from 0.92 over 120ms, and the word morphs over 180ms. The Working glyph breathes to 45% on `--breath`.
- **Activity dot:** an 8px dot. Working is info blue and pulses on the 1.2s loop, Needs you is warning amber with a ping ring on the same loop, Idle is muted at 40%, Failed is solid crimson and still, Unreachable is a hollow ring, Sleeping is a moon glyph. The colour moves between states over 280ms; a shape change cross-fades from 0.92 over 120ms.
- **Task ring:** 16px (12px in a board row), wound from the top as a clock is read, drawn in `currentColor`. With no plan it turns a 30% arc continuously (one turn per 1.2s loop, linear). With a plan, the arc length eases to the share done over 260ms. Finished, it swaps for a success check from 0.6 over 160ms. Nothing planned and nothing running draws nothing.
- **Thinking indicator:** two 20px marks, a circle and an infinity loop, stacked in one cell and trading places on a three-breath loop (6s), the circle widening and flattening as it fades and the infinity settling out of a narrower, taller shape. Beside them four words ("Thinking", "Moonwalking", "Planning", "Refining") each hold for two breaths of an eight-breath cycle, rising 80% in and out on ease-out, while a band of strong ink crosses each word once a breath. Everything moves by transform and opacity on the compositor. With reduced motion it shows the infinity mark and the first word, still.
- **Tab icon:** Caw on a rounded `spark` tile with transparent corners, 4px at the 16px a tab draws (`tab-icon-r`; the 64px bitmap scales it to 16px), the same tile in every state. Where it shows Caw it shows his head, through a fixed box of his 512 still box; his body cannot be read at 16px. It moves only for what the operator has to look at. Something needs the operator (the Needs-you queue): he waves a wing beside his head, the one state that moves, through a 350-unit box that holds his head and that wing. Otherwise something is working: the plain app icon, cut to the tile's corners, still. Otherwise his head asleep and still, through a 280-unit box so it fills the tile: the icon the page is served with. Every picture is drawn ahead, so showing a still runs nothing. Waving, his drawings change 12 times a second, the pace his loops are held at, and keep changing while the tab is in the background, where the icon is the only part of the page still seen. A change of state swaps the picture at once, with no tween. With reduced motion every state is its still.
- **Thinking steps:** a column of steps joined by a 1px hairline connector that draws down as each step starts (160ms). A step's glyph cross-fades in its cell (120ms), new steps fade in over 140ms, and the active label shimmers on `--breath`.

### Transcript
- **Rows:** a row arrives by opening its place first, from nothing (or from the height of the row it replaced) to its measured height over 180ms on ease-out. A rail row's 2px rail draws down with that opening as one stroke. Then its content fades up 3px over 140ms. The rows above make room on the same clock. A row leaving folds shut over 280ms unless another takes its place in the same update, in which case the newcomer tweens from the old height. There is never a frame with the place empty.
- **The reader's message** opens on the composer's clock (120ms, ease-out) because the room it takes is the room the composer gives back, and its words fly in from the field.
- **Live tail:** one row holds the turn's indicator, reasoning and answer in turn. A phase change cross-fades inside the row (out 160ms, in 140ms) while the row's height tweens over 260ms on ease-in-out.
- **Compaction divider:** a 1px wavy line in `brand-solid` across the column (1.5px amplitude, 16px wavelength) with Caw's head and "Compacted" in `brand-ink` at its centre; the whole row is the button that opens the compaction's brief under it. Under the pointer or keyboard focus the line and word step to `brand-ink-strong` and a chevron arrives after the word over 160ms on `ease-arrive` (4px slide, scale 0.25, 4px blur, opacity), leaving over 120ms on ease-out; the centre makes room by moving and the arms by clipping, never by resizing. Open, the chevron stays, turned down over 160ms on ease-in-out. A press scales the centre to 0.97, never the line. Landed live, the line draws in once from the centre over 260ms on ease-out and Caw comes in with it from 0.9; read back from history nothing moves. Caw is still otherwise.

- **Subagent:** a disclosure head (26px, 44px on a coarse pointer) with a 16px identity mark, a chevron that rotates 90° over 120ms, the tool name in mono, and a status pill in meta. Its transcript sits in an 8px recessed well; its report is a raised 5px card with the tile shadow.
- **Floating tags** ("Jump to latest", "Catching up…"): a raised 10px tag with a hairline and the overlay shadow. They rise 8px and grow from 0.96 as they fade in over 280ms, and sink out over 160ms. A live dot beats beside them.

### Composer

The textarea is 16px Figtree at 1.25 leading with no visible scrollbar. It grows from one line (34px) to a 200px ceiling over 120ms, and its scrolled edges fade over one line.

### Permission gate
A raised card (12px, control edge, hairline shadow, 11px padding) floating over the composer. It arrives once, rising 8px over 240ms, and is then completely still: the arrival is the whole signal.

**The Consequential Grant Rule.** A standing grant ("Always allow … for …") is a wider grant than the request, so it is kept apart: under a 32px break and a hairline, after a sentence saying what it would allow, and drawn in the warning tint with warning ink, a shield glyph and a real edge. ### Identity marks and tiles
- **Identity mark:** a 26px square on the board (18px on a transcript speaker line, 16px in a subagent head, 17px for the reader's own mark at 4.6px radius) at 5px radius in a `mark-N` colour under the sheen, with the vendor glyph in `mark-glyph`.

### Icons
**The Solar Duotone Rule.** Every product icon is Solar's bold-duotone cut, imported through `$lib/icons.ts`: one face for the whole product. The duotone's second layer is the same `currentColor` at 50% opacity. Chevrons and arrows (`alt-arrow-*`, `arrow-*`) use Solar's linear cut because they are glyphs, not marks. Close, Minus, Plus and Tick are local glyphs drawn to Solar's 24px grid with a 1.5 linear stroke and round caps, because Solar has no bare version.
- **Sizes:** 12px beside meta text, 16px in controls and rows (the default for any unsized icon), 20px in nav and headers.

### Focus and hit areas
**The One Ring Rule.** Every focusable element shows one ring on keyboard focus only: a 2px solid outline in `focus-ring`, 2px outside the element. Text fields, list rows, tabs, options and anything flush against a container or inside a clipping scroller carry the ring inset on their own border box instead, so it never stacks on a border or gets cut. The ring's colour is set at rest, so focus only switches it on. A scroller brings a focused control into view with room for its ring. Pointer focus shows nothing.

**The 44 Touch Rule.** On a coarse pointer every control's tappable area reaches 44×44 through a transparent `::after` (`.touch-hit`, WCAG 2.5.8), while its drawn size and the layout stay the same. Where neighbours sit closer, `--hit-gap-x` and `--hit-gap-y` stop each area at the midpoint so areas never overlap; clusters open their gap on touch (8 to 14px for a row's controls, to 16px in the top bar). A scroller that would clip the areas pads into an equal negative margin. Menu items and nav rows are 44px tall on touch.

**The 24 Pointer Rule.** On a fine pointer, a control drawn smaller than 24×24 reaches 24px through `.pointer-hit`, with the same midpoint stops. `--hit-scale` keeps the area at 24px on screen inside a zoomed canvas.

**Hover only where it exists.** Hover styles sit under `(hover: hover)` or a fine pointer, so a tap never leaves a stuck hover. The pointer-driven ghost never appears on touch.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, radius, type role, spacing step, curve and duration from the tokens in `design/tokens/cawco.tokens.json`. Add a token there and run `bun run tokens` before adding a one-off value.
- **Do** keep the action coral with its top-highlight gradient, and spend other hue only on status, identity and threshold data.
- **Do** check every change in both schemes; neither is the default.
- **Do** measure white on any new coral fill to 4.5:1, top gradient stop included.
- **Do** recess the frame and raise the content; the stat well is the only card-in-card.
- **Do** say status with a glyph or a word before hue, and draw idle as no chip at all.
- **Do** set every changing figure in tabular numerals (`.num`), and keep weights at 400 and 500.
- **Do** use Solar bold-duotone for every product icon, linear only for chevrons and arrows, at 12, 16 or 20px.
- **Do** make a compact control scale to 0.97 on press and a wide or grouped one tint to `surface-fill`.
- **Do** give every touch control a 44×44 area and every small pointer target a 24×24 area without changing the drawn size.
- **Do** enter on ease-out over 260 to 280ms and leave over 160ms; move things already on screen on ease-in-out.
- **Do** carry shared objects across a change, and reflow lists rather than letting rows jump.
- **Do** put every travelling motion under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` (or `motionOk` in script), and keep fades for reduced motion.
- **Do** show a skeleton at the real layout's size while data loads, and the empty state only once the data is known to be empty.
- **Do** keep Approve and Deny as equal peers at opposite ends of their row, with the standing grant set apart below.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use coral for a status, crimson or spark for an action, or introduce indigo anywhere as a brand or accent hue.
- **Don't** use a hue for decoration, or use crimson, spark yellow, olive or blue for anything but status.
- **Don't** set text on `brand-solid`, turn a tooltip or ink tile coral, or put spark, paper or crow ink in product chrome.
- **Don't** set product text in Fredoka, or code in anything but JetBrains Mono with ligatures off.
- **Don't** write a token value in `app.css` or a component, or edit the generated `tokens.css`.
- **Don't** set any text above weight 500, or pick a size without a role.
- **Don't** use `rgba(0,0,0,·)` shadows; shadows come from the warm ink tints.
- **Don't** use an ease-in curve, or let an exit run longer than its entrance.
- **Don't** keep an arrived surface moving: no pulse or attention loop on anything that is not live work.
- **Don't** draw a focus indicator as a box-shadow, a border-colour change or a background, and don't stack a ring on a border.
- **Don't** truncate on the control or link itself, which clips its hit area.
- **Don't** render a text field under 16px on a phone or a coarse pointer.
- **Don't** swap Solar for another icon set or for SF Symbols, or drop the duotone layer.
- **Don't** use pill-shaped buttons; full rounding is for state pills, switches and dots.
- **Don't** put a third control between Approve and Deny, or make one more salient than the other.
- **Don't** show an empty state while data is loading, or claim zero while the hub connection is not live.
