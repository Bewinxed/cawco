# Visual foundations

The fixed list `DESIGN.md` answers, every question, in this order (Claude Design's create-design-system list, "answer ALL these questions", with the roles and scales a token file holds). An answer says what the project does, the tokens it uses, and where you read it (`file:line`, a URL, a brand file). A question the material leaves open goes on the decision page; it is never answered with a default.

1. **Colour roles**: which colours the project has and what each is for (surfaces, ink, the action colour, states, borders); how each changes in the dark scheme.
2. **Backgrounds**: what fills a page, a panel and a raised surface; flat, tinted, gradient, texture or image.
3. **Type**: each face and what it sets (display, body, labels, code); the scale from the largest to the smallest size; weights; line heights; letter spacing; casing.
4. **Spacing**: the steps and the rhythm between them; how space changes with the viewport or container.
5. **Radii**: the corner scale and which elements take which step.
6. **Borders**: widths, colours and where a border is drawn instead of space or a shadow.
7. **Shadows**: the shadow system, its levels of elevation and what sits at each.
8. **Transparency and blur**: where see-through surfaces or backdrop blur appear, and how much.
9. **Motion**: durations, easing curves, what moves and what never does; the reduced-motion answer.
10. **Hover and press states**: how a control answers hover, press, focus, disabled and error; the focus ring.
11. **Imagery**: the photographs, illustrations and icons: their style, crop, treatment and icon family.
12. **Cards**: what a card looks like: its surface, border, radius, shadow, padding and how cards sit together.
