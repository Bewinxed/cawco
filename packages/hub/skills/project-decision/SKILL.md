# A decision card that is yours

The project's Who decides gives you a kind of choice, so the cards the send names wait on you, not on the person. The send gives each card's question, the writer's why and the options with what each gets, costs and builds.

1. **Read what decides it.** The project's files, its memory, the brief the card came from. Done when one option clearly serves the product best, or you know which fact is missing.
2. **Settle each card.** Call `settle({ card: "<page>#<card>", option, why, sources })`: `why` is one sentence naming the fact that decides it, `sources` the links or paths it rests on. The person can pick again. Done when every card the send names is settled.
3. **Say nothing else.** The writer's waits settle with the card; no message to it is needed.
