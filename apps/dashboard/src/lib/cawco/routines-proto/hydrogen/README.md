The Routines first view, drawn by the `designing-first-views` skill from tsk-442's "Outcomes first" brief (origin: Gemini Hydrogen's bento, `cawco/80224d8f`).

1. One list of outcomes, a row each: a fact the project depends on (with its answer: Yes, No, Not yet or Not known) or what its recurring work delivered, beside one sentence of what was found or delivered, and a Needs you mark with Fix with Caw only when its routine stopped or its newest run failed. Those that need him lead, then the order they were made in. Under a 640px list each row is a card: the outcome, the mark when it needs him and the open button, with the answer under them.
2. Opening an outcome (its open button) shows the routine behind it: its latest run's whole result, what it does and when, what it waits for, and its earlier runs; Edit and Change with Caw sit at its head.
3. Opening a run shows its whole result and, where a session did it, the session.
4. The editor holds a name, what each run does, an optional "only run when", and the schedule.
