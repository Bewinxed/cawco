# Composer

The owner's picks for the composer (`transcript/Composer.svelte`).

- **The Composer Layer Rule.** Nothing that comes out of the composer moves the transcript. The slash and @ menu, the delegate tray's shelf and panel, the plan, the recall wheel and the grown shape are layers over the transcript's foot, anchored to what opened them; the transcript's box, scroll and rows hold still while one opens or closes.
- The plan ring on the tray row wears the needs-you dot while the plan holds away notes the person hasn't seen; opening the plan takes it off on every device.
- A pen swiped up on the pill grows it into a canvas (the artifact panel's brief).
