# Decision page

The owner's picks for a decision page's cards (`decisions/DecisionPage.svelte`, the kit's `.dp-card`).

- A press on an option picks it, kept at once, and the card stays open. There is no Continue, no Done and no fold.
- The page's own Send, at its foot, is the only Send: the panel's head and a Needs you row have none for a decision page.
- A note is read for intent: one with no pick is the person talking, answered first and then asked again.
