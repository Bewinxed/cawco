# Artifact panel, comments, canvas and ink

The owner's picks for the session's artifact panel (a page, a preview, a canvas, a component canvas), its Comment mode and its ink. The PRD (`.design-foundations/plans/2026-10-06-projects.md` §5.10) specifies the surfaces; DESIGN.md holds the system they stand on. Decision logs: `decisions/commenting/log.md`, `decisions/comment-card-sizing/log.md`, `decisions/app-comment-mode/log.md`.

## The panel

- The panel stands on the conversation's own background with no separator line: only its shadow parts it from the transcript, and the split's drag edge shows its grip on hover or focus alone (pin on title-bars-close, 2026-11-03).
- **The head** (2026-11-05, one screen with the rail; HIG Toolbars, Item groupings): one row. Leading, Close on a phone's sheet (HIG Sheets: "for sheets with a single view, the Cancel button belongs on the leading edge of the top toolbar"), then the artifact's **title**; in the centre the mode; trailing, one glass of bare symbols (Comments as its bubble and count, Reload, Open in new tab, Expand, More), Send as the one prominent action on the coral, and Close in a glass of its own beside the page (HIG Toolbars: "Group navigation controls and critical actions like Done, Close, or Save in dedicated, familiar, and visually distinct sections").
- **The head floats as glass** (the owner's pick on the panel's decision page, 2026-11-06, "Looks right" on desk and phone): no bar behind it, its groups glass capsules over the card's ground in the head's room (`c-head-room`), the page standing under that room (SideSurface `float`). The page never runs under the glass: it is another document, whose own top a head over it would cover, so nothing scrolls beneath and no scroll edge effect is drawn.
- **The title** is the words its agent wrote, never the slug, which is an id (the owner's answer on title-bars-close, 2026-11-05). It is the HIG's bar title, sized as the HIG sizes one: "Headline | Semibold | 17 | 22" (HIG Typography, iOS Large), set as plain text in the row role (16px at 500, the house's ceiling), with a menu chevron beside it because it opens the document menu (HIG Toolbars: "Leading edge. … followed by the view title. Next to the title, the toolbar can include a document menu"). The glass group across from it stays at its 36px capsule; the title was too small and in the wrong form (13px in a chip), the buttons were right. Its ask stands under it in the bar, a smaller line, each one line cut at its end (the owner's pick on the panel's decision page, 2026-11-06: Title over its description, a window's subtitle), and is the menu's first row and its tooltip. The head's priority rule gives the title its room before Expand, Reload or Open and before the mode widens to its segments (head.ts: the name takes the rest of its width once Send, the mode and Comments stand); on a phone it takes two lines in the bar's row, so an agent's title of ordinary length reads whole at 390 ("Where Close stands in the glass bars": Close, the title in two lines, the mode as one button, Comments and More). Why two lines rather than a cut: the HIG asks a bar's title to stay "under 15 characters long so you leave enough room for other controls" (Toolbars), and an agent's title runs to 80; a second row would be the second band the owner refused. The artifacts menu's rows take two lines too; the menu is wide enough for an ordinary title in two lines (384px, 320px on a phone), the row on show wears the selection wash, and no row carries a Remove at rest: the menu's last item, "Remove from this session", removes the one on show ("Remove this artifact? This can't be undone."), and Delete or Backspace on a highlighted row asks for that one by its title, each asked in the menu under its last item. A question's Cancel takes the focus with no ring after a press (The One Ring Rule: keyboard focus only). On a phone the title's chevron stands inline after its last word, and the head's groups stand 8px apart under a finger (their touch areas meet, never overlap). The Comments button says its count only while the rail is shut (the rail's segments say every count while it stands); its name says "2 to check" while comments wait on him.
- **The mode** (the owner's pin, 2026-11-05): its own glass, each segment a bar item of it, the chosen one in the selected wash: the kit's BarSegments (main 1bbff2235), mounted as ModeSwitch; the Plan | Preview and Assistant | Preview switches, words rather than symbols, stay the kit's segmented groove on the bar's ground. On a desk it stands in the centre (HIG Toolbars: "Common, useful controls appear in the center area", Freeform on iPad); where the head has no room for its segments (a phone) its one button is the first symbol of the trailing glass, beside Comments and More (HIG Toolbars, Item groupings, iPhone: "controls for tool selection and the More menu grouped on the trailing edge"), never a glass of its own nor a bottom bar: the page's own foot bar, Changed since you looked and the Comments sheet hold the foot, and Interact is the rest state, so a tool bar there would cover the page always.
- **Every control in the head's and the rail's glass is the kit's BarItem** (2026-11-06, the rebuild of the bar parts on main): Close, More, Comments, Reload, Open, Expand, the mode's one button, Inspect, the title (`form="title"`, two lines with its description, `lines`), the rail's Search, + and Sort, Cancel and Changed since you looked in words (`form="words"`). Each takes the glass's contour by its place (leading, middle, trailing, only), and hover, press, focus and selected draw on that one box; a press tints. No raw `data-bar-item` button and no kit Button or Toggle stands in this screen's glass.
- **Comments in the head** (the owner's pin 2, 2026-11-06: "the hover highlight has no padding from the left, and has lots of space on the right; the icon is miscentered and missized"): its neighbours' size, its bubble at the bar's symbol size and centred, the same inset on both sides; the open comments' count is the kit Badge's corner form on its top trailing corner (ui/badge `corner`, a 16px ink pill), taking no room, dressed `attn` while comments wait on him and hollow while he holds comments to send; comments not reaching the agent draw the bubble in the fail ink; ⇧C's hidden pins a closed eye on the foot corner. More's count is the same badge, never a glyph and figures beside it.
- **The phone's Comments sheet is the app's drawer** (the owner's pin 3, 2026-11-06: "this drawer on the phone isn't how vaul drawers look on the phone"): edge to edge on the room's foot at both stops, its top corners rounded with a hairline along the top edge, the raised surface, the house grabber; never an inset floating glass card. What it holds brings no ground of its own (pin 4: the rail's square white panel inside it): the rail draws its ground only beside the page and not floating.
- **The Comments float beside the page** (the owner's pick on the panel's decision page, 2026-11-06, Beside the page): a glass panel under the head's room, inset from the card's edges (RailFloat), its corners the card's (`c-float-panel-r`), so its bar's capsules stand concentric in them; the page stops short of it (`c-rail-float-room`) and the card's ground runs on beneath the glass. Neither the page nor the rail draws a scrollbar beside the glass (the owner's note on two stray scrollbars): the rail's list scrolls behind its edge fades, and the page's own bar is left undrawn (overlay `data-cawco-bare-scroll`, a decision page's scroller and foot too).
- **The Comments badge says one count in its own look** (review, 2026-11-06): To check's count in the needs-you pair, else the held count hollow, else the open count in ink; the name says every count. A count wider than one figure keeps its leading end on the glyph's top trailing corner and grows toward the trailing side, lifted half the glass's inset past the item's top, on no more of a neighbour's glyph than a one-figure count (kit Badge `corner`; glyph-place).
- **One mark for "current" in the panel's menus**: the kit's radio dot; the artifacts menu is a radio group of its artifacts, the one on show checked, no accent fill.
- **The rail's search field is the bar's capsule** (InputGroup `look="bar"`, HIG Search fields), its Clear at the capsule's trailing end.
- **A thread on a phone opens the sheet at half its room** (2026-11-06, the earlier whole room reverted), its pin's pill and element lifted above the sheet so what the comment is about stays in view, and another pill there takes a tap; a drag takes it to the whole room. A comment chip's thread in the transcript, with no page above it, opens at the whole room (ThreadSheet `tall`).
- **A control whose question or card stands is pressed** (BarItem `open`): Comments while its rail or sheet stands, never while the sheet stands aside for a composer; a card's Delete while its question stands.
- **A spot is named as a person names it** (second review, 2026-11-06; the owner on names: "slugs are for slugs, not humans"): its label and what it is ("Search comments field", "Usage limits card"), read by the overlay off the element as the page draws it (agent spot-name.ts, the anchor's `name`), never a component's name or the element's text run together. A row's headline is that name at the body's size and 500, leading the row; the comment's words under it in the row ink.
- **A thread card's title is its bar's title control**: on a desk in the bar's leading room on the card's text column; in a phone's sheet under the bar at the sheet's width. Close leads every view of a sheet, Back beside it in one glass; a desk card's Close trails (title-bars-close: sheet Close leading, cards keep theirs).
- **A chosen row keeps its levels**: the hover's ground with a hairline ring, never the selected wash; its hover is the row's, kept while the pointer is on Resolve.
- **Fields from the rail's bar unfold under it**, inside the rail at its width: Send all's note and the whole page's composer alike, one at a time; the rows draw no scrollbar, so nothing truncates anew as one opens.
- **The title is never hidden** (the owner's pin, 2026-11-05, reverting "the title stands aside for the page's own": "didn't i say the title of the artifact should be there"): the head always says the title its agent wrote. A page that says the same title leaves its own out under the head instead: the host's hello says `headed` (the panel's head names the page; an inset pane has none), the overlay marks `:root[data-cawco-headed]`, and a decision page's title head (not a setup page's, which renames the project in place) is hidden under it.
- **Bar symbols are Solar's linear cut** (the owner's pick on the panel's decision page, 2026-11-06, Solar Linear), every bar's in the product: each symbol in a bar's glass is its icon's `…Linear` twin (packages/icons, golar house/bar-symbol-linear), the duotone kept for status, tiles and rows. They are one size (the owner's pin on the sheet's bar, 2026-11-05): Close spans 15 of 24 units and Plus 18, beside Solar's 20, at one 1.5 stroke.
- **Menus hang from their bar** (the owner, 2026-11-05: as Caw's card hangs from his glass): the artifacts menu, More, the mode's one-button form and the rail's Sort menu hang from their glass as one shape with it (BarMenu, HangCard), never detached under the control; the title draws no glass at rest and its join is its ground while its menu hangs. `house/no-detached-bar-menu` refuses a detached one.
- On a phone the drawer covers the composer while it is open, the Comments sheet over it too: the composer under it is inert and comes back as it was when the drawer closes. An ask raised on the composer is the one thing the drawer makes way for.
- Three modes, kept per session: Interact (the page takes every press), Comment and Draw. Comments float over the page; they never take its room. Comment's glyph is the pin (a press pins a comment), never a bubble: the Comments toggle beside it is the bubble. Every More entry carries its glyph, Inspect's the tuning one, so their words start on one line.
- On a phone a dialog that opens or closes inside the drawer (the design graph's states, a confirm) leaves the drawer at its stop: the drawer is put back where it stood when bits-ui rewrites its style.
- A component canvas's head says the canvas's description in words, never its path or URL, and never repeats its title; a section waiting to draw says so, and a section that failed says why in place.

## The One Target Rule

On a page in Comment mode, coral is the target and nothing else, and no mark on the page is wider than 1px.
- **Rest:** the element under the pointer takes a see-through tint, `c-target-tint` (`brand-solid` at 8%, its text still 4.5:1 under it), at its own radius, named in the overlay's label.
- **Chosen** (its composer open, its pin open, hovered in the rail or stepped to): a 1px `brand-solid` line (`c-target-stroke`), on its border box or `c-target-gap` outside a bare one, never touching its glyphs. The page's root takes a dashed line inside the document's edge.
- One target at a time; the composer at the element is a text field, so it draws no ring (The Caret Rule). The one 2px coral left on the page is an open pin's own ring. A mark that is the only cue holds 3:1 against the page.

## Comments

- A press in Comment mode writes nothing: it opens the chat composer beside a hollow pill at the element. Esc or a press outside leaves no pin; words or attachments park as a draft on the hollow pill.
- A comment may be a picture or a file alone.
- The pill is the tail-corner pill, 28px tall (`c-pin`), roomier than a bare figure.
- A comment goes to the session by itself a few seconds after the last mark, with no Send ("Go by themselves", 2026-10-23, replacing "On Send"). The page's own Send carries its picks alone.
- **Ink goes only with its comment** (the owner's pick, 2026-11-11: "Only when you comment on it"): a drawing reaches the session only through its own Comment, with his words on it, its picture going with that comment. Ink with no comment stays on the page: never delivered, never pictured to the agent, never counted as owed, so a sketch he forgets stays a sketch.
- **Held comments** (the owner, 2026-11-05: one key sends a comment now, another holds it, one act sends everything held, so he can review a whole screen before the agent starts): ↩ and the send glyph still send at once; ⌥↩ and Hold (in words, before the send glyph, the controls taking their own row under the field) hold it, in the comment's composer and a thread's reply alike (GitHub's Add single comment beside Start a review). A held comment waits as the owner picked on commenting (2026-10-26): its pill hollow, its row marked Unsent, the head's bubble wearing a hollow ring and its name saying "3 unsent". Send all is the rail's one prominent action, trailing its bar ("Send 3", at the glass's height, the bar's other symbols folding into More while it stands); pressed, a note to the agent unfolds under the bar with Cancel and "Send 3 comments" (⌘↩). Held words live in the doc (`ThreadEntry.held`), so they outlive a reload, leaving the page and closing the panel, and the session reads none of them (comment_read, artifact_read) until Send all. A reply sent at once on a held comment waits with its comment. The hub's one delivery, once quiet, carries what Send all let go with the note.
- One breath everywhere: a pin's live dot breathes on the shared breath, never a loop of its own.
- A row in the rail shows its attachments as a strip of up to four 20px thumbs, then +N.
- The Comments sheet on a phone layers over the page: the page never moves, resizes or scrolls under it.
- **The rail's bar** (the owner's pin 5, 2026-11-06, replacing the title menu: Apple's sheet bar is Close leading, the title centred, the confirming action trailing, HIG Sheets; a dropdown hid the other lists until it was opened): one row. Leading, Close on the phone's sheet only; "Comments" as the centred title (Label, 13px at 500); trailing, one glass with Search, a comment on the whole page and Sort (a menu: Newest first, Top of page first). While comments are held, Send all is the trailing action, the one prominent one, and Search, the whole page's comment and the orders fold into More leading (beside Close on the sheet), its menu hanging into the rail: Send beside the three left the title no room at its centre at the rail's 320px or the sheet's 368, and the title stays in the bar's centre in every state (HIG Toolbars: an overflow menu for what doesn't fit). Under the bar the lists stand as one segmented control at the rail's width, each with its count (To check while what changed waits, Open, Resolved, Held while he holds any), in the sheet and the desk rail alike (fluid-tabs `fill`), as main had tabs; a list that empties hands its place to Open. Search (or `/`) turns the bar into the field with its Clear and Cancel in words beside it (never a second ×), the segments standing aside while it does (HIG Search fields: in a top toolbar search "appears as a toolbar button. When someone taps it, it animates into a search field"); Esc clears, Esc on an empty field folds it back. On a desk the rail has no Close: the head's Comments toggle opens and shuts it. The empty state is "No open comments" alone, and on a phone the sheet then opens low (a fifth of its room), never half of it empty. A search looks in every list, never the one in front alone: its hits stand under the name of the list each is in ("Open 4", "Resolved 2"), and every row marks what it was found by (its place, its words, its last line), its words starting near the match when the match stands past what two lines show or in a reply. What waits on him comes first while Newest first stands (an unread answer's row says "New answer ·" on its last line); Top of page first keeps the page's order whole. A row's pill stands in a cell that holds a three-figure pill (`c-pin-cell-w`), so every row's words start at one x.
- **The phone's Comments sheet** is a resizable sheet (HIG Sheets: "Include a grabber in a resizable sheet"): the kit drawer with its grabber and two stops, half its room where it opens and the whole room, dragged below the lower stop it goes, as Close does. A thread opened while it stands opens in it, over its list, with Back leading (the chevron alone, "Back to Comments") and the thread's glass groups trailing; Back is the list again, and Close or a drag down puts the whole sheet away (HIG Sheets: "Display only one sheet at a time"; the owner's pick on the design graph, one sheet at a time on a phone). The page is told the sheet covers it while a thread stands in it, so that thread's pin is brought out above it. A whole-page comment's composer still stands the list aside. Beside the page, the + unfolds the whole-page composer under the rail's bar, as Send all's note does, never over the page's top; its target is a chip with no menu when there is nowhere to walk it out to.
- **The thread card** (2026-11-03; its bar from the title-bars branch, cawco/f2b16f38): no header band. One bar row: Back leading where it opened from the Comments list, then the pin's pill; trailing, Resolve, Delete and Copy link as one glass group (More only while it holds two things, Pin it somewhere else joining Copy link) and Close in its own. Under the bar the element's name, the card's whole width in two lines at most, and "on …" where it says more than the name: the title stands put while the thread scrolls under it (the owner's pick on comment-card-sizing: "Its title and the reply box stay put while a long reply scrolls between them"), as a large title stands under its bar, never squeezed beside the groups. Delete's question unfolds under the bar where Delete was pressed. The thread still opens at the newest reply's first line (the same pick), which the title over it now names.
- **A thread placed again in a sheet** (2026-11-06): Pin it somewhere else drops the sheet holding the thread (the pushed one and the phone's) to its low stop, its bar, title and "Tap the page where it goes." with Cancel in view, the page above it taking the tap; the whole room again once placed or cancelled. The notice's action (and Try again) is a small outline button, never a coral link beside Reply's send.
- **Where a thread opens:** beside its pin, wide enough to read a long reply, growing taller as it needs up to the well's height, where it can be read whole; it grows out of the pill as the top bar's card grows out of the glass. It opens at the newest reply's first line, never jumps as the page scrolls, and takes the keyboard only when the person shows an intent to reply. Its foot says "Sent to" the session; no speaker line stands in the author's place.
- **Changed since you looked** (pick B, with C's chip and a ring in place of the green fill): each thread carries its change lines, the rail's first tab is the inbox of what to check, the agent's turn carries a chip per spot it changed, and a changed element wears a ring, never a fill. Its tag (‹ "5 changed since you looked" ›) is one glass capsule floating over the page at the well's foot, centred (ChangesTag), never a row of its own, so the head stays one row and the page keeps its height: Liquid Glass floats its bars over content, and Safari's Find on Page steps its matches at the foot. It never stands on the page's first block (it once floated at the top, on the heading); the page is told the box it covers, so a stop stepped to is brought out from under it, and the room under its foot (`cawco:foot-room`), so the page scrolls that much further and its last line comes clear of the tag. It stands above the page's own foot bar (a decision page's Send), which the page tells the pane (`cawco:page-foot`), and on its tour it keeps its noun ("Change 1 of 5"). A phone's Comments sheet covers it while the sheet stands; its list's To check holds the same changes.
- A `comment_reply` or `comment_resolve` row is a tool call whose chip opens the comment's thread to read and reply.

## Canvas and ink

- A pen swiped up on the composer's pill grows the pill into a canvas; Dock flies it into the panel and Undock back. Ink can be drawn on any artifact, and its pen menu offers two halves: Comment above, Erase below (PRD §5.10).
- **The head offers no New canvas** (the owner, 2026-11-07: "i would never open a new canvas from there"): a canvas is made from the composer (a pen's swipe, Draw, ⌘⇧D), never from the panel's glass or its More.
- **A canvas stands under the head, never beneath it.** Its stage is a layer on the body over every page layer (CanvasStages), so it would cover the head's glass where a page in a frame runs beneath it: the panel's canvas slot starts at the head's foot, and the head keeps its title, its description and Close over any canvas, empty or not yet loaded.
