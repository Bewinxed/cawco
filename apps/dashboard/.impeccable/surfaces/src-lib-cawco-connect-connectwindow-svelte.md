# Add machine

The owner's pick for adding a machine (`connect/ConnectWindow.svelte`).

- Add machine is a modal, the one sanctioned exception to The Open Page Rule: the kit's dialog over the scrim on a desk, its bottom sheet on a phone.
- Dismissed while it works, it minimizes into the machine's place and opens again exactly where it was (The Work Outlives Its Surface Rule).
- Its two ways in are tabs, Over SSH and By a command, over the details, with no question first.
