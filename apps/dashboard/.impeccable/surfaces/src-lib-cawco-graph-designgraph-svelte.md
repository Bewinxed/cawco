# Surface brief: the design graph

**Mode:** Operate. **Job:** a page inside the product, in the artifact panel; it keeps DESIGN.md's visual system and lays itself out for its task. **Verdict:** scrap (spec.md §5), then streamline (the critique of 2 November 2026), then streamline again for one coherent layout (the critique of 4 November 2026, 26/40: the parts each designed alone, composed into one grid), then streamline the Animation face from its tasks (4 November, second pass: each step of T3, T9 and T18 served by one control, the rest cut); this brief describes the surface as built.

## Who arrives, and why
The owner of the fleet, from an agent's one-line ask ("Judge the design graph on the routine form at Save failed"), for a look of a minute or two, many times a day: at a desk on a wide window with the agent's conversation beside the panel, sometimes on an iPad or a phone. He comes to see the asked state on the real component, step through its other states, compare two, see the whole set and its holes, play the move into a state and tune its timing, try a list with one, many and the longest records, and leave remarks the agent reads back. Nothing to press when done.

## The two rules every part serves
1. **The stage is the one surface.** The states, the whole set, the move and Compare annotate the stage: a row of the move rested on rings its thing on the stage; a difference named is a link to its ring, and the stage brings that ring into view; the room takes room beside or below the stage, never over it, and the stage's scroller ends at the room's edge at every stop, on a phone too.
2. **One model every face reads.** One timeline scale, from the move's start to its end; one path order for the steps (the component's, as the list shows it); one kept state and one state before it (where the move into it starts, else the one before it on the path), the same in Compare and the Move face; one close; each width named once.

## The grid (4 November 2026)
- One page gutter (`--space-6` on a desk, `--space-5` under the desk line) holds the bar and the panels. The bar's title starts on the panels' leading edge (its menu's hover hangs out past it); its glass ends on their trailing edge.
- The stage and the room are two panels of one kind: `rounded-lg`, `border-hairline`, `shadow-tile`, a raised head of `c-top-bar-h` over a hairline, sharing a top and a foot. The stage's head holds the kept state's name in the title role at the panel's inset (`--space-4`); the component's page under it keeps the same inset, so the name and the drawing share one leading edge. The room's head holds its faces and its Close on the same head line.
- The stage holds its drawing and no more (Stage `need`: the drawing's width and the panel round it); the room takes the rest, so the map and the timeline get the width a form held to its measure leaves. With one state the room keeps its least. The room's share follows the stage until the person moves the edge (paneforge takes `defaultSize` once).
- Every settings card and inspector is one pattern: a value's name leading in its own column, its control trailing, every control one field high. A clip's settings are the exception the task asks for: a line of setting chips under its row, each opening its choices.

## Parts (by job)
- **The bar**: one row, DESIGN.md's Liquid Glass title bar. Leading, the component's name (a menu of the others when the ask names several). Trailing, one glass group of bare symbols: Compare and the stage's view; Show states only while the room is put away. The view is a card hung from the glass (HangCard, its `settings` frame: as wide as its rows), three rows on one two-column grid, each its name leading and its control trailing: Width as segmented items, each a device glyph then its figure on one line (390, 680, 1440), the component's own marked on the listed width it equals and standing apart only when none does; Appearance as sun and moon in the same segmented control; Reduce motion as a switch. No tooltip opens over the card. Focus a press hands back draws no ring.
- **The stage**: the real component mounted live with its states file's records, always 1:1, at its own width and height: no box wider or taller than the component (a form held to its measure takes that width, and the room takes the rest). It shows the kept state and nothing else, named at its head; it changes only on a press. A state set in place lands whatever the component's own handlers did with the one before: it is read against the state drawn afresh (hidden, its layers portalled into it) and mounted anew where its words or its controls' states differ; a component a states file draws follows its props (house/follows-props). Its scroll is kept across presses and moves; on open it stands at the drawing's top, the component's head whole at every width; as the phone's drawer moves to another stop, the region the kept state changed stays in view above it, with room for its ring. Rings stand in a layer of the stage's own, taking no press.
- **The room**: one secondary surface with three faces on one fluid-tabs switch: the states (the default), the whole set, the move. One close, its own, and Esc. On a desk the stage and the room are one resizable split (the kit's Resizable, paneforge, on the app's own split edge): the edge between them drags, the stage never under 600px and the room never under 400px; the stage's pane stays while the room comes and goes.
- **The states (the journey)**: every state under its machine's section head, one list row each with its name and its one-line note; a note cut at two lines reads whole in the house tooltip. The kept row is the house list's selected row (DESIGN.md, Selected): the `selected-bg` wash under its words in selected ink, so coral reads as chosen, never as an alarm (4 November, second review). The list opens with the kept row in view and a machine's head whole at its top. At its foot the steps along the component's path: Previous, "9 of 11, Failed save", Next; the steps belong to the States face alone.
- **The whole set**: a map that pans and zooms (Svelte Flow, dagre): the room's raised surface, each machine a recessed group, each state a raised card with its name, marks and note. Every move is an arrow routed round the cards, the moves between machines included, labelled by its act where no card stands; solid when the states file plays it, dashed when the agent only named it. It opens fitted to every card by Svelte Flow's own fitView with the house's fit (flow/fit.ts `FIT`), never under the zoom at which the cards' notes (the meta role) draw at 10px: a set too big for the room at that zoom stands at it on the kept card (FlowAutoFit `focus`), the rest a pan away, the view held inside the set's bounds (a gutter round them; the pan stops there too). It fits again in place as the cards are measured or the room resizes while the view is still a fit. The fit tool frames the same way, named "Fit at a readable size" (second review: fitted whole, its words drew at 6px); no zoom figure. Map handles say nothing to a reader; each arrow is named by its two states and its act. In a pane under 640px the map is a list (DESIGN.md, Map mode): each machine under its head in the caps role, each state its card at the column's width with the moves out of it, opened on the state on stage. The kept card is the chosen card, the coral edge (DESIGN.md, Map mode); error and hole marks are each their status glyph then its word ("Error state", "Unreachable", "Dead end"), the whole sentence in the house tooltip. A hardening case (the longest or the shortest text) is a variant of the state it stresses, never a state of its own.
- **The difference**: Compare, a switch that stays on and leaves the room open. A changed value's line gives the kept state's value first, as the drawings stand, the arrow pointing from the compared one into it ("Menu “When”: weekdays ← day"); an empty side is said in words ("emptied (was …)", "filled in: …"). Where the stage holds both drawings at their width with `--space-6` between (the room closed, a narrow component, a wide window) the two stand side by side, each named over it, what went ringed dashed on the compared one; elsewhere "Compared with X" is a press that shows X in the kept state's place. The differences lead with their count ("4 differences"), a press that shows or folds the lines: open on a stage a desk's least wide (`c-graph-stage-min`) or wider, folded on a phone's, until he chooses. Parts pair by what a person calls them (and their element) where one part on each side holds that name, before their place, then by words; a decoration hidden from a reader is no part. A field is named by its label, a menu by its field's label, a kit wrapper by the one control it holds; what came in or changed is ringed on the drawing, what went is a line with the dashed mark and no ring (its box is in the other drawing). It compares with the state before the kept one; a press on another state compares with that one. Each difference is one line at the stage's head and a link to its ring; turning on brings the first ring into view.
- **The move** (4 November 2026, second pass: designed from the task model's T3, T9 and T18, each step served by one control; the run record of that pass lists them; Motion Studio and Jitter the references). **Head**: one line of one height (`h-c-btn-h`, never wrapping; the title gives way first, Reset all its glyph on a narrow face): the move as the face's title, a menu of every move the states file names under it (DropdownMenu, the bar's title pattern with its chevron; long names wrap); a press on one plays it from its from state, as does a press on a map arrow. Once anything changed, trailing: **As written | Yours** (the house segmented ToggleGroup; Yours with the count of rows he changed) and **Reset all** (ghost), which leaves **Undo reset** in its place until anything changes again (undo over confirm). Under the rows, a key names each bar colour on the face (its swatch, then Size, Position, Opacity, Colour, Effect or Other). The moves, a clip's lengths and its curves are one list (ChoiceList: rows padded so long names wrap whole, the pick ticked), each opening on its pick; the curve list stands as tall as its room and brings Bounce into view as Spring is picked. As written draws and plays the agent's timing, read-only; Yours his. **Transport**, one group at the ruler's corner: Play, Repeat (house Toggle: the move goes round again each time it ends) and the time held over the length, as wide as its widest reading; with reduced motion, "Reduced motion: lands without playing" after the time. Pause holds the stage on its frame (each replay paused where it stands). **Ruler**: bits-ui Slider, a press or drag scrubs, during a play too, and the move stays held where it is let go; a pointer let go within half a mark of either end lands on that end's state. Its keys are the face's own: arrows 10 ms, Shift or Page Up/Down a mark, Home and End the ends. Its handle a flag (`ink-strong` pill) at the ruler's foot; under a finger the ruler is `c-btn-h-lg` tall, so the flag's area never covers a bar. **Rows**: one per named part that moves, in the order the engine ran them untuned, read down two fixed columns: what moves and how, in one width for every move and both versions (`c-graph-move-name-w`, 192px), a changed row's restore glyph at its end inside that column; its lane (463px of a 696px room). No length column: a bar's length is its width on the ruler, and its figure is the Length chip's (Motion Studio shows none). **One press per row** (his words: the row's words and its chevron opened two different things with nothing saying which): the row's chevron stands at its leading edge inside its one press, and the press opens or closes the row, the chevron turning a quarter, the words in the house Selected pill (coral is the open row's alone), the bar ringed `ink-strong`, its thing rung on the stage where it stands at the move's time. An open row shows its settings under it and, where its thing moves more than once, a row per run and property under those (motion `partsOf`), named in words (`words.properties`: Opacity, Position…; a custom property said from its own name, `--swap-away` as "Swap away"), its run and its parts' count, each opening its own settings with its own chevron; closing an animation's row leaves its row open, closing a row closes all of it; a press on a closed row's bar opens it. Names stand in `minmax(c-graph-move-name-w, 38%)`. Settings, in place under the open line: three setting chips (ui/chip `setting`, each a Popover): **Delay** (its own delay, a field with − and +), **Length** (the design system's durations by name with their figures, then Own length typed), **Curve** (the chosen curve drawn over the list with two control points to drag, then the list: the house curves, Spring with a Bounce slider, the Agent's curve, named and drawn, from its frames where a transition keeps it there, while it is none of them, and Your curve once he bent one). Length offers one choice per length under every name it carries. In As written the rows are muted words with no press, a line under them saying they are read only. No curve numbers, control points, shapes, intensity, mass, stiffness or damping (the brief: a control no step needs goes). **Edited**: a changed value's chip is followed by the restore glyph (IconReset, ghost icon-xs), named for what it goes back to; a changed row's length figure turns `ink-strong` with the restore glyph before it (the whole row back); the original timing stays a dashed outline under the bar. No dashed chips. **Bars**: each run its own interact.js bar; a press selects; the body drags its delay on 10 ms steps of the delay, an end its length on the duration ladder, either settling on another bar's edge within `--space-2` unless ⌥ is held; keys (arrows 10 ms, Shift 100 ms, Home, End) move it and play nothing. A committed drag or a pick plays the move from that row once. Under a finger a bar's ends grip outside it (`space-7` wide), so the whole bar is the body's. **Scale**: the lane reaches the ruler's next mark a quarter past the move's end (or its end as run), and only grows while one move is on the face, so a drag follows the pointer and a key or a version switch never rescales it; after a drag past the end it grows for the next one. **Narrow** (the face under `--container-timeline`, the phone's drawer, a room at its least): each row's words and length on one line, its lane the face's full width on the next; the transport over the ruler. **Times**: clips are placed by their animation's own start time on the document timeline, so animations set off in one frame share one moment. **Every move's tuning** is kept for the page's life, put back on each new read of that move, and all of it sent as the `tuned` record.
- **The remarks**: every stage element, state, variant and clip row is a named comment target; no Send, no Done.

## His picks on animation-face-questions (b7f01436)
- **playhead-rest: "On the move's end".** After a play (and after the move plays as a state arrives) the playhead rests on the move's end over the kept state, the time reading its length; Play goes round again from 0. With reduced motion, Play lands on the end too.
- **curve-draw: "The list with a curve to drag".** The Curve chip keeps its list and draws the chosen cubic curve over it (CurveGraph, inside CurveChoices' `bend`), its two control points dragged or moved with the arrow keys (0.05, Shift 0.01); a bent curve is "Your curve", sent to the agent as his own, off the design system's tokens. A spring (its Bounce) and a curve kept in a transition's frames (the Agent's curve, drawn from its frames) have no points to drag.
