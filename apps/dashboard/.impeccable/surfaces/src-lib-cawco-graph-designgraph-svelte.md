# Surface brief: the design graph

**Mode:** Operate. **Job:** a page inside the product, in the artifact panel; it keeps DESIGN.md's visual system and lays itself out for its task. **Verdict:** scrap (spec.md §5), then streamline (the critique of 2 November 2026), then streamline again for one coherent layout (the critique of 4 November 2026, 26/40: the parts each designed alone, composed into one grid); this brief describes the surface as built.

## Who arrives, and why
The owner of the fleet, from an agent's one-line ask ("Judge the design graph on the routine form at Save failed"), for a look of a minute or two, many times a day: at a desk on a wide window with the agent's conversation beside the panel, sometimes on an iPad or a phone. He comes to see the asked state on the real component, step through its other states, compare two, see the whole set and its holes, play the move into a state and tune its timing, try a list with one, many and the longest records, and leave remarks the agent reads back. Nothing to press when done.

## The two rules every part serves
1. **The stage is the one surface.** The states, the whole set, the move and Compare annotate the stage: a row of the move rested on rings its thing on the stage; a difference named is a link to its ring, and the stage brings that ring into view; the room takes room beside or below the stage, never over it, and the stage's scroller ends at the room's edge at every stop, on a phone too.
2. **One model every face reads.** One timeline scale, from the move's start to its end; one path order for the steps (the component's, as the list shows it); one kept state and one state before it (where the move into it starts, else the one before it on the path), the same in Compare and the Move face; one close; each width named once.

## The grid (4 November 2026)
- One page gutter (`--space-6` on a desk, `--space-5` under the desk line) holds the bar and the panels. The bar's title starts on the panels' leading edge (its menu's hover hangs out past it); its glass ends on their trailing edge.
- The stage and the room are two panels of one kind: `rounded-lg`, `border-hairline`, `shadow-tile`, a raised head of `c-top-bar-h` over a hairline, sharing a top and a foot. The stage's head holds the kept state's name in the title role at the panel's inset (`--space-4`); the component's page under it keeps the same inset, so the name and the drawing share one leading edge. The room's head holds its faces and its Close on the same head line.
- The stage holds its drawing and no more (Stage `need`: the drawing's width and the panel round it); the room takes the rest, so the map and the timeline get the width a form held to its measure leaves. With one state the room keeps its least. The room's share follows the stage until the person moves the edge (paneforge takes `defaultSize` once).
- Every settings card, editor and inspector is one pattern: a value's name leading in its own column, its control trailing, every control one field high.

## Parts (by job)
- **The bar**: one row, DESIGN.md's Liquid Glass title bar. Leading, the component's name (a menu of the others when the ask names several). Trailing, one glass group of bare symbols: Compare and the stage's view; Show states only while the room is put away. The view is a card hung from the glass (HangCard, its `settings` frame: as wide as its rows), three rows on one two-column grid, each its name leading and its control trailing: Width as segmented items, each a device glyph then its figure on one line (390, 680, 1440), the component's own marked on the listed width it equals and standing apart only when none does; Appearance as sun and moon in the same segmented control; Reduce motion as a switch. No tooltip opens over the card. Focus a press hands back draws no ring.
- **The stage**: the real component mounted live with its states file's records, always 1:1, at its own width and height: no box wider or taller than the component (a form held to its measure takes that width, and the room takes the rest). It shows the kept state and nothing else, named at its head; it changes only on a press. Its scroll is kept across presses and moves; on open it stands at the drawing's top, the component's head whole at every width; as the phone's drawer moves to another stop, the region the kept state changed stays in view above it, with room for its ring. Rings stand in a layer of the stage's own, taking no press.
- **The room**: one secondary surface with three faces on one fluid-tabs switch: the states (the default), the whole set, the move. One close, its own, and Esc. On a desk the stage and the room are one resizable split (the kit's Resizable, paneforge, on the app's own split edge): the edge between them drags, the stage never under 600px and the room never under 400px; the stage's pane stays while the room comes and goes.
- **The states (the journey)**: every state under its machine's section head, one list row each with its name and its one-line note; a note cut at two lines reads whole in the house tooltip. The kept row is the house list's selected row (DESIGN.md, Selected): the `selected-bg` wash under its words in selected ink, so coral reads as chosen, never as an alarm (4 November, second review). The list opens with the kept row in view and a machine's head whole at its top. At its foot the steps along the component's path: Previous, "9 of 11, Failed save", Next; the steps belong to the States face alone.
- **The whole set**: a map that pans and zooms (Svelte Flow, dagre): the room's raised surface, each machine a recessed group, each state a raised card with its name, marks and note. Every move is an arrow routed round the cards, the moves between machines included, labelled by its act where no card stands; solid when the states file plays it, dashed when the agent only named it. It opens fitted to every card by Svelte Flow's own fitView with the house's fit (flow/fit.ts `FIT`), never under the zoom at which the cards' notes (the meta role) draw at 10px: a set too big for the room at that zoom stands at it on the kept card (FlowAutoFit `focus`), the rest a pan away, the view held inside the set's bounds (a gutter round them; the pan stops there too). It fits again in place as the cards are measured or the room resizes while the view is still a fit. The fit tool frames the same way, named "Fit at a readable size" (second review: fitted whole, its words drew at 6px); no zoom figure. Map handles say nothing to a reader; each arrow is named by its two states and its act. In a pane under 640px the map is a list (DESIGN.md, Map mode): each machine under its head in the caps role, each state its card at the column's width with the moves out of it, opened on the state on stage. The kept card is the chosen card, the coral edge (DESIGN.md, Map mode); error and hole marks are each their status glyph then its word ("Error state", "Unreachable", "Dead end"), the whole sentence in the house tooltip. A hardening case (the longest or the shortest text) is a variant of the state it stresses, never a state of its own.
- **The difference**: Compare, a switch that stays on and leaves the room open. The differences lead with their count ("4 differences"), a press that shows or folds the lines: open on a stage a desk's least wide (`c-graph-stage-min`) or wider, folded on a phone's, until he chooses. Parts pair by what a person calls them (and their element) where one part on each side holds that name, before their place, then by words; a decoration hidden from a reader is no part. A field is named by its label, a menu by its field's label, a kit wrapper by the one control it holds; what came in or changed is ringed on the drawing, what went is a line with the dashed mark and no ring (its box is in the other drawing). It compares with the state before the kept one; a press on another state compares with that one. Each difference is one line at the stage's head and a link to its ring; turning on brings the first ring into view.
- **The move** (4 November 2026; round 29 the idea, Motion Studio and Jitter the references): one grid of three fixed columns, one row per named part that moves (its control, else its closest `data-slot` a reader is told of; a morph text is one row named by its words) in the order the engine ran them untuned, so a retimed row never moves; every row one line of `c-btn-h`, a `border-hairline` between rows. Columns: what moves and how ("Form failure" in `ink-row`, "unfolds" muted; hugging its words up to 40%, cut with the whole in its tooltip; a chevron after it when the thing moves more than once); its length, a tabular mono figure ending on one edge (the animations' one length when they share it, as the editor's Duration says, else the span; `~` for a spring); its lane. A ruler row on top: Play and the time ("0 / 861 ms") over the first two columns, the ruler over the lane. The move starts where it first set anything off (each clip's start less the earliest), so the first bar stands on 0. Each bar in its kind's wash and edge (`c-clip-*`), `c-badge-h` tall, a thing that moves twice drawn as both stretches joined by a hairline. One scale: the lane runs to the ruler's next mark past the move's end, the time past the end shaded `surface-recess`; ticks, figures, gridlines, bars and the playhead all on `t / scale`; the shade, the gridlines and the playhead are drawn inside each row's lane, so they stop at an opened editor; the playhead's handle a short `ink-strong` pill at the ruler's foot (bits-ui Slider; a press on the ruler scrubs). Each run of a row (the clips that ran together, read as the engine ran them) is its own bar, and a gesture or a key moves that run and no other (his objection, 4 November: dragging one run of Create routine moved both). The pointer is interact.js 1.10.28 (MIT), one interactable per run: a press does nothing, the body drags its delay, each end resizes it, by touch too (`touch-action: none`); one rule for a drag and a key (motion `moveRun`: the snapper, 10 ms on the body and the duration ladder on an end, freed with ⌥; clamps modelled on DialKit's `clampClip*`). Body and ends are sliders of their own (arrows 10 ms, Shift 100 ms, Home, End); the ends are not bits-ui's Slider, whose track takes a press. A bar too narrow for its grips stands its ends outside it and, under a finger, gives its width to the body. A row of several runs names each run's bar ("…, run 1 of 2"), its length figure is its whole span, and each animation opened under it says its run ("Run 2 fades"). Play plays, then seeks, so the drawn stagger is what plays. A press on a row's words opens its editor under it (his pick), the words then in selected ink. An edited length is marked on itself in the row (coral, dashed edge, "Reset length to 260 ms"), pressed to restore. The head holds the move's name and, once anything is edited, Reset (every value back). The editor (`bg-surface-recess`): rows of name | control: Duration (a field with − and + inside it and "ms", c-graph-figure-w; Quick, Standard, Slow as one segmented control beside it; the design system's name for the length after them), Delay (the same field), Curve (one list, the house Select with group headings: the design system's curves, then the shapes, each with its glyph), the curve drawn under it with its two control points, Intensity or Bounce as a slider, Changes ("height 0 → 120 px") when the clip changes one number, Advanced (a disclosure in row ink with its chevron) holding the curve's four numbers or a spring's mass, stiffness and damping. A value he changed is marked on itself: its figure in the edit's ink, and its name a press, dashed underneath, that puts that one value back (a property inspector's changed label). Coral means edited or selected and nothing else on the face.
- **The remarks**: every stage element, state, variant and clip row is a named comment target; no Send, no Done.

## States the surface holds
Reading/mounting (skeleton at the stage's size after the wait grace); failed to mount (error formula in the stage's place, Show details); one state, no machine; kept state with no move; the move reading or failed; playing, paused, scrubbed; comparing, a difference brought into view; a value edited (its chip marked, the original outlined, Reset in the head), a bar dragged by its body or an end, a spring, a move of 30 minutes, German figures; reduced motion (Play draws the end with a fade); the hub's link lost (the stage keeps its drawing, marked stale, dimmed).

## Widths
- **390**: where the surface cannot hold the stage at its least (600px) and the room at its least (400px) side by side with their gap and gutters (1058px of its own width), the room is the kit's bottom drawer, stops head, half, whole; the page ends at the drawer's top at each stop; the grabber steps the stops; the drawer's own Close; every control 44px to a finger.
- **680**: the same drawer; the stage never covered.
- **1058 to 1440**: stage and room in view at once, both inside the surface, the stage at its drawing's width (never under 600px), the room taking the rest.
- **2529**: the surface has about half the window; the stage takes its component's own width, the room the rest, no paper between.

## His answers (spec.md, His answers)
A full move editor, presets first, advanced for any length, curve and spring; a tap keeps, and that is the look; the move plays once on arrival then rests at its start; one component in front, the others behind a switch; Compare is a switch that stays on; the journey opens at the asked state; records as they stood when the agent opened the surface, with that time named. Later, on the preview: the stage changes only on a press; resting never previews. On design-graph-arrangement: on a desk the states and the move stand in a room beside the stage, the one arrangement.

## Answers in the handoff of 4 November
He asked for one coherent layout, as a strong product designer would draw it in one pass: controls inside their containers, one direction per card, the stage lined up with what is round it; the Animation face usable (no stray marks, nothing clipped, the axis on 0, hierarchy, no useless buttons, no jump on a press); an edited value marked as changed on itself, a press restoring it.
