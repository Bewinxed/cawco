Privacy policy

CawCo is made by Petra Solutions LLC. This page covers what data CawCo handles, where it goes, how long it's kept and how to have it deleted.

Last updated October 8, 2026

Your work stays on your machines

You run the CawCo hub and dashboard yourself, on your own machines. Your agent sessions, transcripts, prompts and files stay there. Petra Solutions never receives them.

When a transcript links to a website, your hub fetches that website's icon directly from the website. It never asks a third party for it.

The iOS app

The app talks to your own hub. To deliver push notifications it also uses CawCo's push relay, which runs on Cloudflare. The relay receives and stores:

  • your device's Apple push token;
  • a random pairing id and a hash of a random secret;
  • the push environment;
  • the App Store transaction the app sends to verify your purchase or free trial.

The relay uses these only to deliver your notifications and to check your purchase. It deletes a pairing 30 days after its last use, when its trial ends, when you remove the device, or when Apple reports a refund.

Notifications

A notification carries only what it takes to tell you something needs you: the session's own name, or which agent on which machine. Notifications pass through Apple's push service on the way to your device.

Purchases

Apple processes every purchase. CawCo receives Apple's notices of purchases and refunds of its products, and uses them to verify your access. It also uses them to count purchases per paywall version. Those counts carry no user or device identifier.

What CawCo doesn't do

CawCo doesn't track you, show ads or sell your data, and the app has no analytics SDKs in it.

Deleting your data

To delete a device's pairing, remove the device in the app or in the dashboard. For anything else, email support@cawco.dev.
