What the critique ranked first

  1. Coral means one thing: the kept state and its move.
  2. The graph names its machines and the state each is in, always readable.
  3. A hover marks on the page what it changed.
  4. The kept move is drawn on the graph, and the dock's playhead rides it.

  6. Notes say why.
  7. A place for your verdict.

Settled for the build

  • Coral marks only the kept state and its move: the kept node and variant, the edge into it, and the dock's playhead. Every other machine's state is grey, and the sliders are ink.
  • The edge into the kept state is lit, and while the move plays a dot rides it in step with the dock's playhead.
  • Edge labels cut through their line, regions share one left edge, and play, zoom and fit use one icon set.
  • Every state keeps its note whole: what it's for and what to check, never its name again.
  • The Showing line is gone: the component starts at the top of the page, and nothing above it moves.
